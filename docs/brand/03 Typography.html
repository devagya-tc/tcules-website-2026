Typography

Three families with three jobs: a serif that speaks, a sans that reads, a mono that labels. Type is set by role, never by size.

Roles

Nineteen roles. Each is a composite of weight, size, leading and family, with a tracking companion where one is needed. On the web, sizes step down at 1023px and 767px. The role never changes; only its size does.

Three caps, in characters of the role's own size. A block of text past its measure is a layout error, not a type error.
