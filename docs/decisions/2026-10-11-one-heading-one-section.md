A band's items stay in one composition, with any closing line inside it. Splitting a band into a photo split plus a stray row of cards, or adding a separate Statement band for its closing line, reads as "2 sections or one?".

**Why.** The reader cannot tell where a section ends.

**The mistake it prevents.** A photo split followed by an orphan row of its own cards; a Statement band carrying another band's last line.

**How to apply.** One heading, one composition.
