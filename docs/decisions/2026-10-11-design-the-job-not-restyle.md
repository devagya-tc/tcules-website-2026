When the owner says to spend time on a section, "identify the content, its IA, the prominence and understand the value of that section and then design for it." Never iterate visual styling on the same layout, and never default to cards.

**Why.** Owner: "why are you focusing on keeping the same layout and just changing the visual styling of the section little by little… you just keep putting them in cards like that's the best thing." What worked (round 6) was naming the job in one line and deriving the IA from the reader's order of acts.

**The mistake it prevents.** Five rounds of the home gateway restyling one card grid.

**How to apply.** For any band under review, write its job and the reader's sequence first, then consider forms other than cards (selector, index, story, comparison). Delegated designers are told this explicitly, and their first output is checked before a critic sees it.
