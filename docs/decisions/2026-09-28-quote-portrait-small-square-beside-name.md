The quote portrait is **small, 1:1, beside the name and role** under the quote (the ch10 "site
blockquote" pattern). No portrait means no gap.

**Why.** The quote is the content; the portrait identifies the speaker. Portraits are 1:1 (ch05 v1.0),
and the attribution is where a reader looks for who said it.

**The mistake it prevents.** A large portrait competing with the quote, or an empty slot left where a
quote has no portrait.

**How to apply.** `Proof` renders the portrait through `Media`, 1:1, beside the attribution; when
there is none the attribution sits alone with no reserved space. `role` is optional. Superseded 7 Oct by `2026-10-07-no-rating-beside-quote-avoid-headshots` (itself superseded 10 Oct by `2026-10-10-testimonials-use-a-rated-card`, which brings the avatar back when available).
