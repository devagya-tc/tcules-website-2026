Type leans to the smaller sizes generally. Targets the reader picks by title are the exception (see `2026-10-15-selector-titles-are-the-targets`).

**Why.** The owner's stated preference in the 15 Oct review.

**The mistake it prevents.** Defaulting each role a step larger "for presence".

**How to apply.** When choosing between two roles for a line, take the smaller unless the line is a target or a hero.
