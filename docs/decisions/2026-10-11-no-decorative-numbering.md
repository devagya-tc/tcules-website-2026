The owner dislikes decorative numbering: no ornamental numerals (01–04) on lists or claims. They prefer uniform content per item.

**Why.** 01–04 numerals added to the listings were "messy".

**The mistake it prevents.** Numbered "why" claims on home and numerals added by the listings critic loop.

**How to apply.** Number items only where the order is real information (steps in a process).
