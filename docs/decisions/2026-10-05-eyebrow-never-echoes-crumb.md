Breadcrumbs take too much room and look like an island because the hero eyebrow repeats them. The eyebrow is a free content slot and must not echo the crumb.

**Why.** The repeated location doubled the hero's chrome.

**The mistake it prevents.** Eyebrow set to the crumb's last item.

**How to apply.** Superseded 11 Oct by `2026-10-11-no-eyebrow-under-a-breadcrumb`: under a crumb the eyebrow stays empty.
