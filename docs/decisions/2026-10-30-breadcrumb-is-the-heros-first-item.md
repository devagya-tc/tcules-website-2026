When a page has a breadcrumb it is the first thing in the hero, above the eyebrow and the title, in every
arrangement. Nothing sits above it and no title sits between it and the page's top.

**Why.** With a long title set across the column (`titleAcross`) beside an illustration, the trail stayed in the
copy column and landed between the title and the lead, which has no logic: a trail says where the page is before
the page says anything.

**The mistake it prevents.** Moving the title out of a column and leaving the trail behind in it.

**How to apply.** PageHero places the trail with whatever holds the title; when the title is across, the trail is
above it.
