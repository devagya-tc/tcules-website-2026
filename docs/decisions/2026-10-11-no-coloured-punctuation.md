No coloured punctuation: quote marks take the text's ink. Blue quote marks were disliked.

**Why.** Coloured marks turn punctuation into decoration.

**The mistake it prevents.** Blue quote marks on testimonials and VoiceLedger rows.

**How to apply.** TestimonialCard and any quoted line. ch02 §7 does not say what ink punctuation takes.
