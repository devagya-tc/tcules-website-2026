A listing index (the case-study index, the insights hub) carries no testimonial band, for now. The
case-study index has no quote anywhere since its cards stopped holding one.

**Why.** "For now lets not add a testimonial to the listing index." The listing's job is to get a
piece opened; proof lives on the case pages and on home's testimonial band.

**The mistake it prevents.** Re-adding the quotes that left the case cards (2026-10-22-case-cards-
carry-no-testimonial) as a band of their own between the list and the close.

**How to apply.** `ListingIndex` has no testimonial slot; do not add one until the owner asks. The
corpus's quotes on `_pages/case-studies.md` still say which case each group features.
