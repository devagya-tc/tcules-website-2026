A composition made for one page stays there: the home case+quote row suits home, and service pages need their own design. Derive the principle, not the layout.

**Why.** Copying a layout copies the answer to a different question.

**The mistake it prevents.** Service pages stamped with the home case row.

**How to apply.** When a home pattern seems to fit elsewhere, carry over the principle (one primary, a quieter secondary) and design that page's band for its own job. Contrast: the same job across pages reuses its iterated pattern.
