Where a claim and a testimonial share a row ("why Tcules" and similar), the claim is primary and the quote visibly secondary: smaller, quieter. Nothing should fight for attention.

**Why.** Owner: "everything is fighting for attention."

**The mistake it prevents.** A quote set at the same weight as the claim it supports.

**How to apply.** Check every band for one clear primary and a quieter secondary before shipping. Applies on every page.
