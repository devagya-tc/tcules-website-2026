An illustration cover uses an archetype from the registry in ch05 §9a, chosen by its decision order
(product UI, then activity, domain, industry, use). A new archetype is added to the registry as a row
before anything is made against it.

**Why.** The registry was the last item left open on 24 Sep and the owner approved it on 25 Sep. A
fixed set of archetypes is what lets covers across a listing read as one family and lets the
"no two adjacent covers share an archetype" check be run at all.

**The mistake it prevents.** Inventing a one-off motif per project, so a listing becomes a row of
unrelated pictures and nobody can say whether two neighbours repeat.

**How to apply.** Before drawing a cover, record its kind, archetype row and modifier with the asset
(§9a "Recorded with the asset"). If no row fits, propose a new row first; never draw outside the
registry.
