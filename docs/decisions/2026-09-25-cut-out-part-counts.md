A cut-out carries up to five parts at full column, three on a single feed post, one per carousel
slide, two in a card (BlockCard), and one in a thumbnail (ListCard).

**Why.** A part has to be legible at the width it is read at. The counts step down with the
placement so each part stays at its own scale; past the count the image is a screen again, and a
screen fails at 326px.

**The mistake it prevents.** Assembling one rich five-part composition and reusing it in a card or
thumbnail, where every part shrinks below the scale floor.

**How to apply.** Author per placement against these counts. A dialog or panel carrying its own edge
counts as one part. A thumbnail's cut-out is one recognisable shape at 160px.
