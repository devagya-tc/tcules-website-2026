Do NOT reduce pages to ~5 sections. The owner rejected the cut.

**Why.** Claude misread "5 sections" in the 1 Oct review transcript as a page-length target.

**The mistake it prevents.** Service pages recomposed with half their bands removed.

**How to apply.** Recompose a page with the kit's components; keep its sections. Length is negotiated through content proposals, not by dropping bands.
