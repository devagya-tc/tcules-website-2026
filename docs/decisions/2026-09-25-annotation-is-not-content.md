Numbered rings over a capture or cut-out, and part labels on an illustration, are allowed. The rule
that no image carries type bans *content* over an image, not annotation.

**Why.** The 24 Sep ruling that no image carries type was broad enough to forbid the annotation ring
and the illustration's concept chip and part labels, which point at parts of the image rather than
say anything of their own. The owner drew the line at content: a headline, claim, caption or
attribution.

**The mistake it prevents.** Stripping the ring and labels out of every image to satisfy the no-type
rule, or the reverse: using "it's only a label" to put a claim over a photograph.

**How to apply.** Rings follow §2 (56px ring, 3px stroke, mono numeral); illustration labels follow
§4b (one concept chip, part labels of two or three words). Nothing else sits on an image. A project
cover carries no annotation (§9a).
