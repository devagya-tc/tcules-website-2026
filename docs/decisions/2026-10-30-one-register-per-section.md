Every visual inside one band is of one register: all flat graphics, all isometric, all photographs or all proof.
"People → IRL photo" applies when people are a band's subject (About, the studio), not to one row of an offer.

**Why.** The studio photo in What you get, among flat drawings, made the band read as parts glued together.

**The mistake it prevents.** Choosing each item's visual alone, so one band mixes a photo, drawings and covers.

**How to apply.** Decide the band's register first (treatment-rules § 6), then draw every item in it.
