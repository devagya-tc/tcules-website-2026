Any card may carry an image, of any type. The image is optional. This overturns ch05's "cards other than case studies carry no media".

**Why.** The owner wants images throughout the pages.

**The mistake it prevents.** Service, audit and glossary cards forced to stay text-only.

**How to apply.** Every card component supports both states. ch05 §9 "Card imagery by family" still says services, audits, glossary and capabilities carry no media, and §12 row 05 records the old rule.
