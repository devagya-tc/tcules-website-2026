No image carries type, on any surface. Words sit beside or below an image box, never over it. This
holds for photographs, captures, cut-outs and illustrations, and for share previews, posts,
carousels, covers, video thumbnails and section openers alike. A share preview or post that is
designed type on a flat ground is not type on an image, and is unaffected.

**Why.** It replaced §6's "Photographs can carry type" and the whole §6a apparatus (permitted rows,
measured region, blur band, plate device). One rule with no exceptions cannot be argued case by case.

**The mistake it prevents.** The photographic section opener ruled in on 16 Sep, and any hero,
card or social frame with a headline, scrim or blur band over a photograph.

**How to apply.** No component sets text over media: `PageHero`, `Figure` and `BlockCard` media are
blocks. Annotation is not content and stays allowed (`2026-09-25-annotation-is-not-content`).
