The owner cannot make judgements on a content→component mapping document. No owner-approval stop goes on abstract intermediate docs; automated critics review those, and the owner reviews rendered pages in the browser.

**Why.** A stop the owner cannot act on costs time and buys nothing.

**The mistake it prevents.** A workflow pausing for owner sign-off on a mapping or spec document they cannot evaluate.

**How to apply.** Workflows run mapping → critic → build → visual critic → owner reviews routes.
