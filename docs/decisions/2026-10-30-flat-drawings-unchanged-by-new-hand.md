The flat drawings (`Sketch` and its kit, the bento's story drawings, EngagementOptions, TracePlates) don't
move toward the new isometric banners' look. They keep the flat grammar
(`2026-10-26-flat-graphics-grammar`): neutral frames, one blue subject, no peridot.

**Why.** "Flat drawings do not change." The two tiers are meant to differ
(`2026-10-26-flat-or-isometric-two-questions`), and the bento's drawings are approved.

**The mistake it prevents.** Recolouring the flat scenes to the banners' blue line and tints for "one
hand across the page", which was proposed in docs/research/illustration-evolution-2026-10-30.md § 5 q4.

**How to apply.** The small-drawing proposal in that document (§ 3) applies to isometric figures only:
Reasons, and anything else the two questions send to the isometric tier.
