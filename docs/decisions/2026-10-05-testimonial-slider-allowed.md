A testimonial slider is allowed, because testimonials are not SEO content. On 7 Oct G·02 "Typographic, with a CtaRow" was the favourite, to go in an auto-advancing slider; the "large image + testimonial" variants are "all good options".

**Why.** Hidden slides cost nothing in search when the content is testimonials.

**The mistake it prevents.** Ruling out the slider for testimonials on SEO grounds.

**How to apply.** The kit's Slider pattern. Cards are a different matter: `2026-10-16-no-hidden-cards`.
