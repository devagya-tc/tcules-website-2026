People use the testimonial card's panel with a square or circle portrait, name, position, bio and the LinkedIn link on a ruled foot. A studio-wide "follow us" goes in one ProfileFollow banner under the set. Never a 16:9 card image for a person.

**Why.** Owner: "i would not call these profile cards."

**The mistake it prevents.** Team members rendered as 16:9 image cards.

**How to apply.** ProfileCard for any set of people.
