Each hard part is one full-width row of three columns: the small claim illustration (168), the claim's title and body,
and the example paragraph exactly as the copy writes it ("Example:" and its inline case link, a normal body link, in the
same body style). Nothing else: no logo, no citation line, no case card, no cover.

**Why.** The band was left-heavy with dead space on the right, and every previous fix invented a new device for the
example (a cover, a case card, a citation with a logo). The copy already carries the proof and its link.

**The mistake it prevents.** Adding a bespoke component to present content the copy already presents.

**How to apply.** ChallengesBand: drawing beside words is a split (section, 96); claim beside example are peers
(column, 64). Phone stacks drawing, title, body, example; tablet drops the example under the body.
