Visitor-facing pages get the full design treatment; pages that exist only for SEO may compromise.

**Why.** Effort goes where visitors are.

**The mistake it prevents.** Spending equal design effort on SEO-only pages, or letting an SEO page's constraints shape a visitor page.

**How to apply.** When a page's design is a trade-off, ask which kind of page it is first.
