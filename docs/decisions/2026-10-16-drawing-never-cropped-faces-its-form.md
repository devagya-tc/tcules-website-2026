NEVER crop the drawing. It must FACE THE FORM (mirrored), which overrides the left-facing habit.

**Why.** A cropped drawing loses its object; one facing away from the form points the eye off the band.

**The mistake it prevents.** Round 7's corner-crop placement of the desk drawing.

**How to apply.** The enquiry close desk and any drawing paired with a component. ch05 §4a lets a mechanically cropping placement crop an illustration.
