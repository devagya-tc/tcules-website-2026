Each hard part is one row: a small illustration of the claim, the claim's title and text, and under the text its
example as a footnote: the client logo (when we have one) and case title as one link with an arrow, the example sentence
beneath in smaller type, its "Example:" lead-in dropped. No case cover, no case card. An example without a case page
keeps only the sentence.

**Why.** Covers or case cards beside an illustration gave each row three focal points and outshouted the claims, which are
the band's content; Selected work already gives the cases their large moment on the page.

**The mistake it prevents.** Promoting the proof to the picture, so the section becomes a second case list.

**How to apply.** Illustrations at a small fixed size (about 168px wide), one focal point per row.
