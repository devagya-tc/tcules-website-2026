A studio member's LinkedIn profile background (1584 × 396, margin 64, ground paper-deep) carries the
monochrome wordmark at 32px, at the right end of the centre band: the one corner of the visible area
the profile photo never covers. The rest is an illustration or one mono line of role context at 32px.

**Why.** The profile photo covers roughly the lower-left 568 × 264px on desktop and more on mobile,
so the mark goes where it is always seen. It is distinct from the LinkedIn company cover, which
carries no wordmark because the company logo already sits over it.

**The mistake it prevents.** A wordmark placed bottom-left under the profile photo, or one carried on
the company cover beside the platform's own logo.

**How to apply.** Use the one template for every studio member; only the illustration or role line
changes.
