"a worked example is different from hard parts, which is different from how we do." Hard parts are claims; how
we do it is a method, the numbered steps beside one flat graphic ("the right component for this content");
a peer set pairing each item with what goes wrong without it is the drawn ledger ("also right for its
content"). A worked example authored inside a hard-parts band renders as its own block after the claims.

**Why.** The owner pointed at /services/product-modernisation's method and /services/design-systems'
status-example as right for their content, and at a worked example sitting inside hard parts as a different
thing.

**The mistake it prevents.** The visual inventory of 30 Oct listing the worked examples as hard parts, and a
renderer choosing one shape for all three.

**How to apply.** Decide the kind from what the content does, then use that kind's component
(treatment-rules § 2). The method (`F`) and drawn ledger (`drawn-table`) stay as built.
