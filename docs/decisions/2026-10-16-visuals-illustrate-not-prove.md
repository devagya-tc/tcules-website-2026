In reference sections like MarkOS, Linear and Stripe, a mini-UI vignette or line figure illustrates the sentence beside it; its numbers and labels are decorative and invented. The content is only a title and a sentence. "The components aren't filled with information as you suggest."

**Why.** The visuals had been read as evidence (stats, client marks, quotes) the content team must supply.

**The mistake it prevents.** Content asks generated from a reference's decorative data.

**How to apply.** When reading a reference, separate illustration from data before writing what it asks of content. Per-item visuals are drawn from each item's argument.
