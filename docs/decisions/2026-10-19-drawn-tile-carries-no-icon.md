A tile led by its own drawing has no glyph: the drawing guides the eye, and the title opens the tile. The owner compared both ("the icons are a bit much but they help guide focus") and chose without.

**Why.** Two blue marks competed in every tile, a glyph above the title and the drawing's subject, and the glyph floated in the wide tiles' corners.

**The mistake it prevents.** The S1/S3 tiles with a Solar glyph above every title as well as the drawing below it.

**How to apply.** Bento. `2026-10-09-icon-heads-its-card` still holds for icon cards with no drawing.
