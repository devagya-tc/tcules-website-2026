A clickable-card section is eyebrow + heading + body + the cards, with the heading above the cards, never beside them, and never a decorative image. Non-clickable item lists may sit beside an image.

**Why.** The boxed 6-card grid with an image beside it was too busy.

**The mistake it prevents.** Linked card grids with a head track or photograph beside them.

**How to apply.** BlockCardSet and any linked set; CLAUDE.md § Design quality carries it.
