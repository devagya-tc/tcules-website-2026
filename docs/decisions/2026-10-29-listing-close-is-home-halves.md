A listing's island close is the one home has: the island split in halves, the sketch-to-shipped drawing
over the heading and lede, the TextureFlow field moving across the island, and the enquiry form in its
card dress (untitled). Not the desk drawing beside the form in the TextureFrame.

**Why.** "Form on the page isn't the right one, the one with the moving texture is preferred."

**The mistake it prevents.** /insights closing on the older framed island while home closes on the
halves, two closes for one ask.

**How to apply.** ListingIndex's `closing.island` (the insights hub today; the case-study index takes
the plain close).
