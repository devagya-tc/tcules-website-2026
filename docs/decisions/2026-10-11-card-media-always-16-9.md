Card media is always 16:9 at every width and placement, so one master size serves cards, banners and the Clutch export. Never per-instance card ratios.

**Why.** One ratio means one asset per item.

**The mistake it prevents.** Cards cropped to different ratios per placement.

**How to apply.** BlockCard and any card with media. ch05 §9 still sets BlockCard media at 3:2 (vertical) and 4:3 (horizontal).
