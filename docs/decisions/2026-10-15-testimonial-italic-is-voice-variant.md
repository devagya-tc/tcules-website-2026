Italic testimonials are a `voice` variant, used only beside content. Testimonial walls are upright.

**Why.** PR #108 made italic the default; the owner reversed it the same day.

**The mistake it prevents.** A wall of italic quotes (PR #108).

**How to apply.** Testimonial and Proof take italic only through the `voice` variant. ch03 §2 defines `--text-quote` as italic by default.
