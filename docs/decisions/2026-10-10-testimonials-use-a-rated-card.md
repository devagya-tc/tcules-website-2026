Testimonial groups use a real testimonial card: star rating, quote, name/role, and an avatar when available. The owner's references are 3-column rated cards and staggered cards beside a heading.

**Why.** A bare quote does not read as a testimonial.

**The mistake it prevents.** Unrated, unattributed quote blocks in testimonial groups.

**How to apply.** TestimonialCard. Placeholder ratings follow `2026-10-19-placeholder-ratings-floor`.
