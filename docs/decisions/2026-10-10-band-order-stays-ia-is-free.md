The narrative's band order stays. Inside a section, build whatever IA serves it. The markdown's layout is NOT a component spec: "quote inside a case" in the source never means a case card with a quote stuffed in.

**Why.** The narrative is the owner's; the composition is the designer's. Reading source layout as structure produced pass 1's literal, ugly mapping.

**The mistake it prevents.** A case card with its quote stuffed inside because the markdown nested them.

**How to apply.** Every `_pages` mapping: keep the sequence of bands, redesign freely within each. The one sanctioned reorder is the enquiry form before the FAQ.
