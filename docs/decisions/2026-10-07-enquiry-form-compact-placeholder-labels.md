EnquiryForm is compact, with no visible labels: the placeholder acts as the label, and the accessible label is hidden. Keep "We reply within a business day".

**Why.** The labelled form read heavy for an exposed close.

**The mistake it prevents.** Stacked visible labels above every field.

**How to apply.** EnquiryForm. The reply line lives in the form only (`2026-10-09-reply-time-only-in-the-form`).
