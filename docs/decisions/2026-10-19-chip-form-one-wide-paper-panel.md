The form always sits on a paper panel. Chips are small (~34 tall). The whole close fits one laptop screen, so the chip form is ONE wide panel split left (name, email, message, send) / right (chips, website), with the heading in a row above. The first-message dress with chips is approved as a documented variant.

**Why.** A form set straight on the island "merges into the bg".

**The mistake it prevents.** A form laid directly on the island ground, or a chip form tall enough to scroll.

**How to apply.** EnquiryForm's chip variant and the CtaSection close.
