A missing cover or band visual is never filled by reusing an existing illustration. Draw a meaningful scene for that item from its argument, or fall back to a photo or placeholder. "I keep seeing the same graphic everywhere now… could you not add more meaningful drawings?"

**Why.** The same scene everywhere stops meaning anything.

**The mistake it prevents.** The six service Sketch scenes keyword-matched onto insight covers.

**How to apply.** Service scenes stay where they belong (Gateway/home, nav). A cover graphic is assigned per item on purpose, never by keyword. The /insights lab band is a placeholder until an illustration is commissioned.
