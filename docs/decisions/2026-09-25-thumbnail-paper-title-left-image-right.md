A podcast or video thumbnail is paper, margin 72. Left: the mark at 40, a series eyebrow at 24 and a
title of three to five words at 128. Right: a 480 × 480 area clear of the duration badge, holding the
guest in a square profile box for a podcast, or an unboxed illustration or one cut-out for a video.
Layout F takes the same composition at 1280 × 670 with a 440 × 440 box.

**Why.** A guest is a profile, so a podcast shows the guest boxed as one. A series is held by its
eyebrow, title position and right-hand treatment; only the title and the image change, so episodes
read as a set.

**The mistake it prevents.** Type over a photo of the guest, or per-episode layouts that never read
as a series.

**How to apply.** Build thumbnails from the one template; change only the title and the right-hand
image.
