The hub is curated, and BY TYPE / BY TOPIC switch the whole view. No separate featured section, no format chips under the hero, no full feed, no in-page filtering. Items show their image when they have one. Type sub-pages are built after claude.com/resources/articles.

**Why.** Two layers of navigation need one switch, not stacked mechanisms.

**The mistake it prevents.** A hub carrying a featured row, chips, a feed and filters at once.

**How to apply.** The insights hub and its type sub-pages.
