The "why Tcules" band holds **four** argued reasons, set **2×2** in every arrangement. Home keeps
`open`, "the one as it is today but the 3x2 becomes 2x2", and `beside` takes the same four 2×2 (head ⅓,
set ⅔) as the arrangement to compare against it. Three and six are still accepted because they fill
whole rows. Everything else in the 16 Oct ruling stands: each reason is a title in the reader's words
and one arguing sentence, each has an isometric drawing of its own claim, and every arrangement fits
one fold at 1440.

**Why.** Jatin's review: "will be 4 items (2x2 grid)". The owner asked to see both arrangements
before choosing.

**The mistake it prevents.** Six reasons on home (PR #115's `open` 3×2), or four reasons in a
three-across grid, which leaves a hole: three and then one.

**How to apply.** `src/patterns/Reasons/`. Four reasons go two across wherever the set has more than
one column. The home placeholder fixture (P48) holds four reasons, and the P48 brief asks the content
team for four.
