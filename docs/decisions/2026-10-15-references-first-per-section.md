Redesign goes one section at a time: gather really beautiful live examples (interactions, graphics, visuals), put the list in front of the owner, wait for them to say what to build from it, then build. Never present a polished build as the first step. The goal is "elegance and beauty… make someone awe".

**Why.** Reviewing polished builds wasted rounds; the owner wants to steer direction early.

**The mistake it prevents.** Opening a section redesign with a finished build the owner then has to unpick.

**How to apply.** Per section: research → reference list (artifact) → owner picks → build. Straightforward fixes from a feedback list are done directly; big redesigns wait for the section queue.
