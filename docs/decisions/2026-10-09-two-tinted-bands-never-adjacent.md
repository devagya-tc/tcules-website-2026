Two tinted bands are never adjacent, the close included: where two would meet, one drops to paper.

**Why.** Two tint grounds touching read as one long band with a seam.

**The mistake it prevents.** A tint proof band running into a tint close.

**How to apply.** Page templates and plans. ch02 §8 already says "Never two tint bands adjacent".
