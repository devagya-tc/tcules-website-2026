On Clutch the logo is the profile-picture logomark file, 400 × 400. Portfolio items take up to five
images each, Clutch's own limit. Each image is a case study's own cut-out, exported at 2560 × 1440
with the part inside the central 4:3. No banner is maintained.

**Why.** Clutch does not publish its crop, so the part is kept inside the central 4:3 to survive
whatever it applies. An item without an image does not display there, so every item carries one.
A banner would be one more asset to keep current for no reader.

**The mistake it prevents.** Uploading whole-screen screenshots or recropped covers to Clutch, or an
item with no image that silently never shows.

**How to apply.** When a case study ships, export its cut-outs for Clutch at 2560 × 1440, part
centred in 4:3, up to five per item.
