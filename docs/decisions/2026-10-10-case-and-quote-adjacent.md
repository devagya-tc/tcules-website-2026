A case and its quote sit adjacent, never embedded: if a quote sits inside the case it might as well be in the testimonial section. Featured cases with their quotes come first; the remaining cases follow as a lighter 2-column list.

**Why.** Four identical rows in a sequence is boring.

**The mistake it prevents.** A case list of identical rows with quotes buried in them.

**How to apply.** Superseded 16 Oct by `2026-10-16-testimonial-case-card`: the diptych "reads as disjointed, and as 3 units".
