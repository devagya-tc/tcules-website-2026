Cards other than case studies carry no media: article, insight, service and glossary cards are
media-less.

**Why.** On 25 Sep the image system was being built around client work as evidence, and a card image
that proved nothing (a stock or decorative picture on an article card) was the failure ch05 exists to
stop. Media-less cards were the safe answer.

**The mistake it prevents.** Decorative images on cards whose subject has no screen or concept to
show.

**How to apply.** Superseded on 28 Sep by Phase 2 ruling C1 (`2026-09-28-which-cards-carry-an-image`):
articles, videos and podcasts carry their cover where they have one. ch05 §12 row 05 now records the
revision.
