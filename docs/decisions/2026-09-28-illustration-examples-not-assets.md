Illustrations can be page heroes, "but not the examples directly. even those examples need to be refined further to show what we really want to." The ch05 examples (Intent Stack, Seam, Cutaway, Closed loop) are a style reference only.

**Why.** They demonstrate the method; they are not yet the quality or message the site needs.

**The mistake it prevents.** Assigning an existing ch05 example as a page's illustration.

**How to apply.** Each service line, audit and article gets a new illustration built from its own brief. ch05 §4c presents them without saying they are not assets.
