Repeated proof and repeated lists are cut: the rating sentence under the logo wall (the badge already says it), a weak stat ("Based in India"), a hero "Selected work: a · b · c" line that duplicates a later case band.

**Why.** Owner: "everything is fighting for attention." A repeated line spends attention twice and weakens both.

**The mistake it prevents.** The rating sentence under the home logo wall and the hero "Selected work" line.

**How to apply.** Before shipping a page, check for a line or list another band already carries. A cut that changes authored copy is logged as a proposal.
