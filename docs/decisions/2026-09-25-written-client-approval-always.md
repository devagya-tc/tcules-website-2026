Client work needs written approval from the client before any public use: shipped or not, credited
or not, and for every caption that names the client. "Always", with no exceptions.

**Why.** The question was when approval is needed; the owner chose the strictest answer. Approval is
one of the two records ch05 keeps (with team consent) because other rules depend on it, and it is
filed with the asset.

**The mistake it prevents.** Publishing unshipped work, or a before image, or naming a client in a
caption, on the assumption that a credit or an NDA lapse makes it fine.

**How to apply.** No client-work asset enters the image register or the CMS without its approval on
file. A caption that names a client is covered by the same approval.
