Images throughout the pages are wanted. Studio photos come from tcules.com/about, and the owner can shoot more. Stock images that fit the image rules are fine as placeholders, from any source, and need no "stand-in" label.

**Why.** The review is internal, and beauty matters more than transparency for now.

**The mistake it prevents.** Image-less pages waiting on first-party photography, or stand-in captions cluttering the review.

**How to apply.** Placeholder imagery during the kit phase. ch05 §10 keeps stock off public surfaces; this is a pre-publication rule.
