An illustration is never placed on a `brandeis-500` (blue) ground, on the website or on social.

**Why.** The illustration system carries one solid blue plane. On a brandeis-500 ground that plane
disappears, and §4a has no palette for blue. Rather than invent one, the owner ruled the placement out.

**The mistake it prevents.** A blue band or a reserved blue social frame with an illustration on it,
where the blue plane vanishes and the drawing loses its structure. The 24 Sep addendum (item 6) had
left exactly this open.

**How to apply.** A `Section surface="blue"` or `Island` on blue never holds an illustration;
choose paper, tint or ink for a band that does. A reserved blue social frame carries type only.
