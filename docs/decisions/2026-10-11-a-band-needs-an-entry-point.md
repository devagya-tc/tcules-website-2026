A section needs an entry point and a path for the eye, not only correct type. The enquiry close takes a bare blue glyph above its lede (no disc), at the owner's request.

**Why.** The home gateway's 3×2 grid was preferred to a list but was "dense and boring, nothing guides my eye".

**The mistake it prevents.** A grid of equal items with nothing to start from.

**How to apply.** For each band, name where the eye lands first and where it goes next before choosing the arrangement.
