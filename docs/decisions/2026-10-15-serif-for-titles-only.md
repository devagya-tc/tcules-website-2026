The serif (Newsreader) does not look good outside titles: never in cards or body. Small text is sans; testimonial card quotes are sans for readability (11 Oct).

**Why.** Newsreader at small sizes and in card copy reads poorly.

**The mistake it prevents.** Serif card bodies and serif quotes in testimonial cards.

**How to apply.** Which title roles are serif is set by `2026-10-19-only-display-h1-h3-serif`. ch03 §2 still lists `--text-quote` as Newsreader italic.
