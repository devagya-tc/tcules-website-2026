In a selector, the option titles ("Launch a new product") are the targets, so they are the largest repeated thing in the band. "Smaller fonts generally" does not apply to targets.

**Why.** They had been set at 15–16px, like labels, in three mockups, and the owner flagged it.

**The mistake it prevents.** Selector options styled as small labels.

**How to apply.** Any selector, index or router where the reader picks an item by its title.
