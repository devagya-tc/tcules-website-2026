For every animated drawing, write the story first, then design its two states, then animate. Rest = the problem, end = it solved, and every move must answer "why would it go there?". One element takes centre stage and moves most.

**Why.** "Move only" does not mean displacing the whole drawing; the owner rejected a blue tile sliding next to a screen for no reason.

**The mistake it prevents.** Motion that makes lines overlap or doubles a line (a layer sliding onto a screen did).

**How to apply.** Prefer drawing a line itself (a trace) over moving it back and forth. Applies to every SituationFigure and any hover-animated drawing.
