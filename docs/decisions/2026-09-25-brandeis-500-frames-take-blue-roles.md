A reserved brandeis-500 social frame takes chapter 02's Blue surface roles: hero white, support and
labels brandeis-100, action white, rule brandeis-400, row number brandeis-300. No illustration is
placed on it.

**Why.** Social and the site share one colour system. Inventing a separate ink set for blue frames
would give the brand two answers to the same surface.

**The mistake it prevents.** Blue frames with ad-hoc inks (a blue action on blue, a brandeis-900
ground used as "dark blue"), or an illustration whose blue plane vanishes into the ground.

**How to apply.** Read ink values for a blue frame from ch02's Blue surface, exactly as the site's
`data-surface="blue"` does.
