"Don't send screenshots." The owner reviews in the browser on the dev server, so a change ends with only the routes to open ("no need to describe in words, just tell me where to look"), plus anything unresolved or a decision needed.

**Why.** The owner is the eye in the browser; crops in chat cost tokens without adding anything.

**The mistake it prevents.** Attaching crops or contact sheets as results, as the 28 Sep review page (and the 28 Sep "visual review via `npm run shots` screenshots, they should not open a browser" instruction) had it.

**How to apply.** Never attach images or promise a contact sheet unless the owner asks. Claude may still take its own crops sparingly to check work. CLAUDE.md's "put the crops in front of the owner" step reads against this ruling.
