Client logos take the boxed logo grid from the Dribbble Enorm reference. The section is at most one MacBook Air viewport tall. Its heading is demoted to eyebrow-size text. The marquee is removed.

**Why.** The logo heading was too loud, and a moving strip hides most marks at any moment.

**The mistake it prevents.** The marquee logo strip.

**How to apply.** LogoGrid. Compose from Figures where it can (`2026-09-25-job-is-not-a-component`).
