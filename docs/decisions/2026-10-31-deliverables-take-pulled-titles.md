"pull words to create titles out." Each What you get deliverable on the expertise pages is titled with words from
its own sentence, and the sentence stays as its body. No new words.

**Why.** The four-part What you get is a set of titled deliverables; untitled lower-case sentences fell back to a
ruled grid.

**The mistake it prevents.** Twelve What you get bands of lower-case statements with no titles.

**How to apply.** The titles are in `_pages/expertise__*.md` (`**Title:** sentence`), listed in
docs/page-build/visual-inventory-expertise.md § 2 and logged in docs/content-changes.md for the content team.
