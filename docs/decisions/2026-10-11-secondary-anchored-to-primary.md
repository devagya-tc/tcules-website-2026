A secondary item shares edges with its primary: top-aligned, same height. A testimonial beside a case shares its top edge; vertical centring reads as floating.

**Why.** A quote hanging beside a case read as "randomly floaty".

**The mistake it prevents.** Vertically centred quotes beside case content on home.

**How to apply.** Any pair in a row: align tops, never centre the shorter one.
