`paper` is the primary ground and `paper-deep` the secondary; between them they carry every routine
post and every high-value content type. `ink` and `brandeis-500` are reserved: one-off uses, each
argued, never a family's default and never a rhythm device such as a hinge or a closer. The hinge
keeps the body's ground.

**Why.** Blue's job is to be seen as brandeis-500, so its use is rare and deliberate. Four grounds
cycling as four families had made colour the feed's rhythm.

**The mistake it prevents.** Ink and blue frames as family defaults or as the hinge in every set.

**How to apply.** Corrected the same day by
`2026-09-24-paper-dominates-hinge-may-change-ground`: the reservation stands, but the hinge may take
paper-deep, ink or brandeis-500. Apply that one.
