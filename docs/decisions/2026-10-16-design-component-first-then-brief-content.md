When the existing content does not fit, design the component first, for beauty, against ideal content, then hand the content team a brief: fields, lengths, image spec. Owner: "design a case study card that has a testimonial with it and focus on the beauty of it. we can then let the content team know what the content should be."

**Why.** The home copy and covers were not fitting the case; holding the design to them kept it ugly. No outside example of this component exists (the owner looked too).

**The mistake it prevents.** Bending a composition to fit current copy, or spending rounds searching for a reference that does not exist.

**How to apply.** For such a component, existing copy and covers are not fixed inputs. The content brief goes in `docs/content-proposals.md` (the case card's is P47). Do not spend rounds hunting for an outside example once the owner says none exists.
