"Any specific content related asks will be addressed later. Our job is to design content-agnostic rn." Content inputs (files, copy, quotes) are deferred, never blockers.

**Why.** Final copy is still in flux (v10 is not release-approved), and the point of this phase is that components and templates do not depend on the content.

**The mistake it prevents.** Tasks marked blocked on a content input such as TESTIMONIAL-REGISTER-V10.csv, or time spent chasing final copy.

**How to apply.** Log content asks under "Content asks" in `docs/TRACKER.md` Parked or in the content-changes doc, and design against realistic stress content: shortest and longest lengths, long names.
