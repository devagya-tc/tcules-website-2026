Never blend BlockCard's borderless tile and the hairline text card into a hybrid. "You merged the borderless tile and the hairline card and came up with the atrocity i see… never forget the 'beauty' factor."

**Why.** Variants were merged for code economy, and nobody judged the result by eye. The borderless tile is shaped by its image; the hairline card is a text card.

**The mistake it prevents.** The 3×2 gateway hybrid: top hairline, mono eyebrow, serif title, grey body, a lone arrow at the foot, loose spacing.

**How to apply.** Before reusing or extending a card variant, check that the result still matches one approved kit look (/design/block-card, /design/kit/*). Never ship a combination nobody approved; a critic squint-tests every card set.
