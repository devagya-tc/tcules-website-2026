When the owner calls one piece of a set good, it is protected: change only what they asked, never rebuild it to fit a set-wide rule.

**Why.** Owner: "the one i said was good you changed it drastically (design systems) and now it looks aweful." Design Systems was one of the two best in round 2; round 3 applied new set-wide rules (left-to-right flow, aspect limit) by reversing, compressing and moving it.

**The mistake it prevents.** Round 3 of the service-line illustrations, which destroyed the approved Design Systems drawing.

**How to apply.** If a new rule seems to conflict with an approved piece, apply the smallest change that satisfies it, or ask first whether the rule applies. Say explicitly which pieces were left untouched.
