A section heavy with important items gets drawn cards in the EngagementOptions shape: a drawing per item on its plate, then the title and body, up to five across.

**Why.** Tiles beside a stand-in photo gave the items no presence.

**The mistake it prevents.** "How Tcules works" as tiles beside a photograph.

**How to apply.** Each drawing is made for its own item, never reused.
