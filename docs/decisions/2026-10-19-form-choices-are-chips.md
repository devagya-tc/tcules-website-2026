A pick-from-options question in the enquiry form is drawn as pill chips (selected = filled), not radio buttons. "Not literal radio buttons but chips… the form needs to be clean and beautiful so the visitor actually feels like filling it."

**Why.** Round 1 used radio rings and tile/list rows.

**The mistake it prevents.** Radio rings or list rows for budget and interest questions.

**How to apply.** Chips with short labels (verbatim long headings make ragged pills), real checkbox/radio inputs underneath, hover as a lift. Mocks: the "Choice Form Directions" artifact; nothing is built until a direction is picked.
