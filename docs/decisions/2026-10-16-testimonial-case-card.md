A home featured case is ONE card with its quote (TestimonialCase): a tint card with a mat; a 16:9 cover (always 16:9, light hairline) beside eyebrow, claim, result and link; the testimonial in its own full-width box inside the card, CENTRED and stacked: stars (no numeral) → full quote → portrait | name / role.

**Why.** The case-beside-quote diptych "reads as disjointed, and as 3 units". The card is one view that holds title, description, testimonial and visual.

**The mistake it prevents.** Spreading quote, speaker and rating across the box ("3 separate items"), which the owner rejected.

**How to apply.** TestimonialCase, built on PR #111; the content brief is P47 in `docs/content-proposals.md`.
