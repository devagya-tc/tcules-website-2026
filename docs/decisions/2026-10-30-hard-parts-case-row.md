A problem paired with a named example is one row: title, sentence and the Example sentence on the start track, the cited case's own cover on the end track ([3,2] at section 96, rows 64 apart). No hairlines, no band-level image. An image about one item belongs to that item; a band-level picture must speak for every item. A cited case's picture is its own cover, never a binding matched by client name.
