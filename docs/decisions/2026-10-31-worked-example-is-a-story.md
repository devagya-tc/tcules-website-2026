"the 'illustration, not client work' should be a more visually designed section. either an animated flat graphic
that shows what's in the content or a few frames showing what we're saying."

**Why.** A worked example is the page's most concrete moment, and as words beside one drawing (or as plain
text inside hard parts) it read as more copy.

**The mistake it prevents.** The ScenarioSplit block: a label and paragraphs, with at most one still drawing.

**How to apply.** Proposed until designed and seen. When promoted it replaces the worked-hypothetical sentence of
2026-10-30-l1-drawn-tables-and-scenarios (words beside one flat drawing); that entry's table and service-drawing
rules stand. 24 pages carry one; the frames for the expertise four are in
