The enquiry form always sits immediately before the FAQ, even though the content puts it last. It is the one sanctioned reorder of a page's narrative.

**Why.** The owner set it as the single exception to "band order stays".

**The mistake it prevents.** A page that ends on the form after the FAQ because the source markdown does.

**How to apply.** Every page template and corpus mapping that carries both a form and an FAQ.
