Every icon card stacks icon, then title, then body; never the icon inline with the title. Icons are sized to the card title they accompany (7 Oct: they were too big).

**Why.** Oversized glyphs out-shouted the titles.

**The mistake it prevents.** Glyphs inline beside card titles, or larger than the title's scale.

**How to apply.** BlockCard icon variants; CLAUDE.md § Design quality carries it.
