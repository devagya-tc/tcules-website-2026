IRL photographs get one shared tone layer; the treatment is still undecided. It is `--media-photo-grade`, read by every photograph Media renders, shipping `none` until the owner picks.

**Why.** One tone makes studio photography from different shoots read as one set.

**The mistake it prevents.** Grading one image locally.

**How to apply.** Media; the treatment is made in Figma (`2026-10-09-photo-tone-made-in-figma`). ch05 §5 says "no tint, no duotone… no filter, anywhere".
