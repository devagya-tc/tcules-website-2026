A byline, dates or a reading time use PageHero's byline strip (credits + detail, dates, readingTime), with values from structured fields (corpus `editorial`, CMS fields), never the authored italic line as written. "We designed an article template earlier that had a provision for this metadata."

**Why.** The kit already solved the job; a raw line bypasses it.

**The mistake it prevents.** An article rendering its byline as an italic paragraph copied from the source.

**How to apply.** Any page carrying a byline, dates or reading time.
