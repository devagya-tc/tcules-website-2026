A project's banner, the image at the top of its own page as `PageHero` media, is composed at 16:9:
the same kind, subject and emphasis as the cover, recomposed for the ratio and never a crop of the
cover.

**Why.** The banner's frame was an open question in ch05 §12. 16:9 was chosen and written into §9a;
the cover stays 3:2 because cards crop it, while the banner sits uncropped at full column.

**The mistake it prevents.** Serving the 3:2 cover as the banner, or cropping it to a wide band, so
the concept loses its subject area or the hero takes a ratio nothing was authored for.

**How to apply.** Every case study supplies two assets: the 3:2 cover and the 16:9 banner. `PageHero`
renders the banner intrinsically, uncropped. A banner is never a whole screen (§9a).
