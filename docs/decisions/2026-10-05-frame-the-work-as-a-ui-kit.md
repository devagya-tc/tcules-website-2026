Frame the work as a website UI component kit, not "build the website". Showcase pages can say "this is what the kit can do" and need not follow current content, but must still be Tcules and read as a product-design studio. The bar: "very very beautiful and elegant", never a plain wall of text.

**Why.** The 1 Oct review found a large gap in finish against the Webflow references. Building the 5–6 core components first, with variations, then composing pages from them closes it.

**The mistake it prevents.** Page-by-page builds that never establish the components, and showcase pages held to placeholder copy.

**How to apply.** Build core components (CTA, testimonials, exposed enquiry form, banners, client logos, service listing) with variations before composing pages. The brand guide may evolve towards a finer, richer result.
