Never combine proof types (logos, testimonials, aggregate rating, Tcules record) in one section, and never show every figure every time.

**Why.** Mixed proof competes with itself; a full set of figures everywhere stops meaning anything.

**The mistake it prevents.** A facts band carrying logos, a rating and every stat at once.

**How to apply.** Approved kit compositions still win: the kit/home logo wall with its Clutch rating stays combined (`2026-10-10-approved-kit-compositions-win`), and the case card holds its testimonial (`2026-10-16-testimonial-case-card`).
