"What you get" and "where we start" bands read as a clear offer: the deliverables are the primary object, designed as one group on one side; the testimonial sits on the other side as support.

**Why.** Equal-weight cards in a row with nothing guiding the eye are "boring".

**The mistake it prevents.** The service page's deliverables as a row of equal cards.

**How to apply.** Offer bands on every service page; OfferSheet is the iterated pattern.
