PageHero gets variants with a rating or social-proof badge in the eyebrow (reference: doodle.com).

**Why.** The eyebrow is a free content slot; a rating there is proof at first glance.

**The mistake it prevents.** An eyebrow spent echoing location.

**How to apply.** PageHero eyebrow slot. On pages with a breadcrumb the eyebrow stays empty (`2026-10-11-no-eyebrow-under-a-breadcrumb`).
