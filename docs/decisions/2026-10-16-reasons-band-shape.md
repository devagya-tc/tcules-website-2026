The "why Tcules" band is `Reasons`: **six argued reasons, each with an isometric drawing of its claim**,
in three arrangements, **`open`, `boxed` and `beside`, all one fold at 1440.** Each reason is a title in
the reader's words and one sentence that argues it. The drawing illustrates the sentence and carries no
data.

**Why.** Picked by the owner across four rounds of mocks. The reasons are an argument, not a set anyone
clicks or compares.

**The mistake it prevents.** Treating the reasons as a card set, or reading the drawings as evidence
content must supply.

**How to apply.** `src/patterns/Reasons/`, one per page; `beside` is head ⅓ beside set ⅔ at 1440 (mock
C2b, locked from the owner's screenshot). Each drawing is made for its own claim.
