"We aren't worried about the CMS at all… our job is to design things visually. If it warrants a CMS change, that's done outside of our tasks. CMS isn't a constraint anymore."

**Why.** The freeze was narrowing visual proposals to fit the existing Strapi shape.

**The mistake it prevents.** Presenting "the field doesn't exist" as a reason something cannot be done.

**How to apply.** Design what the page should be, then report the content-model changes it implies as downstream information. The `rich-text` split parked under the freeze is unparked.
