Cards have no boxes: no box around cards or around text. Structure comes from spacing and composition. Images keep their hairline, because an image is a box already and needs separation from the ground. Other visual components may use boxes.

**Why.** Boxed cards were part of the gap in finish against the Webflow references.

**The mistake it prevents.** Bordered card grids.

**How to apply.** BlockCard and any card set.
