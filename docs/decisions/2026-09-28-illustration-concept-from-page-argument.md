Build each concept from the argument its own page makes (read the page, not just its hero line), as a message about what the actor does or decides. Concepts (meaning, message, layer-by-layer story) are written and approved before drawing. The Intent Stack is the north star: "complexity in simplicity", "the strength is not in the number of elements but the ideas shown by them".

**Why.** Round 1 was "far too simple in drawing and carr[ied] almost no meaning"; "the concepts could be 10000x better."

**The mistake it prevents.** A diagram of one plane and a screen that says nothing specific to its service.

**How to apply.** Every hero illustration. ch05 §4d starts from "the idea" but not from the page's argument or a concept approval step.
