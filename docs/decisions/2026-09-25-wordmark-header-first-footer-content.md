Every social frame carries the wordmark once, monochrome: letters neutral-950 on paper and
paper-deep, white on ink and brandeis-500, never the colour-bar version. It sits header-left at 40px
on a first frame (a carousel cover, a single post) and footer-left at 24px on a content frame
(inners, hinge, closer). An uncomposed photograph is exempt.

**Why.** A first frame is met cold, so the account is named before the argument starts; inside a
set the mark moves to the action row and gets out of the way.

**The mistake it prevents.** The mark moved to a margin corner, resized to fill a gap, shown twice,
or in the colour-bar version on a social canvas (never-list 08).

**How to apply.** Templates fix the two positions. The LinkedIn company cover carries none, and the
profile picture is the logomark (D1).
