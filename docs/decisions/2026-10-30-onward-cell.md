Seven services stay on four tracks, 4 + 3. A closing paragraph authored as questions each answered by
linked services is the ONWARD cell, in the eighth cell: a dashed edge (beyond this set), no texture, no
drawing, no hover, not one link; each question over its answer, links in place, words verbatim. A
closing paragraph of another shape keeps the plain text cell.

**Why.** The owner: "it needs to be designed and not just placed." A solid tile reads as an eighth
service; unframed text reads as dropped in.

**The mistake it prevents.** A text cell placed in the grid, or a solid tile that reads as one more
item.

**How to apply.** `Bento` `onward` takes `{ question, answer }` routes; `Router` in OfferBands derives them from the closing paragraph when every question is answered by links.
