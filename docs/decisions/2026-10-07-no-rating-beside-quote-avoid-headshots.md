Never put a rating beside a client quote. Avoid headshots; when a quote has no image, it shifts to the start and carries a tag.

**Why.** Testimonial variants had too many competing elements.

**The mistake it prevents.** Rated, portrait-led testimonial variants.

**How to apply.** Superseded 10 Oct by `2026-10-10-testimonials-use-a-rated-card`, which puts the star rating and the avatar back in the testimonial card.
