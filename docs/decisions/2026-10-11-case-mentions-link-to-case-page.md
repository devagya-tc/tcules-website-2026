Every mention of a case study links to its case page: the corpus page under /case-studies/<slug> when one exists, otherwise a flagged placeholder link.

**Why.** A named case the reader cannot open is a dead end in the argument.

**The mistake it prevents.** Case examples on service pages rendered as unlinked text.

**How to apply.** Any band that names a case. Paths come from `src/lib/routes.ts`.
