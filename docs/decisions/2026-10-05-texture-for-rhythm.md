Texture is not used anywhere yet; use it for rhythm and beauty, wherever it improves page rhythm, with placement and shape decided by real graphic-design judgement. Texture means the iterated generated texture (`Texture` component, `npm run texture`), NOT the ch05 illustration dot grid.

**Why.** Unused, the texture added nothing; the owner wants it carrying rhythm.

**The mistake it prevents.** O3's "at most one per page, never a rhythm device between bands", and confusing Texture with the illustration dot grid.

**How to apply.** Texture component. ch05 still bans any treatment on or behind an image.
