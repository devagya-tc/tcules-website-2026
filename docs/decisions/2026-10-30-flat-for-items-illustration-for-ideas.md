A flat graphic (the kit Sketch grammar) is item-scale: one small drawing per item in a set of four or more
concrete things (deliverables, services, engagement options), showing a mechanism or an artefact. An isometric
illustration is band-scale: one large drawing per band, or one each for two to four peer claims, showing a
concept or an argument.

**Why.** Flat graphics had spread to every band (deliverables, tiles, engagement, then hard parts), so the page
read as one visual register and its drawings as one set of motifs (a card with lines, a blue highlight, a check
badge, an arrow to a person).

**The mistake it prevents.** Reaching for a flat graphic because it is the cheap drawing to make.

**How to apply.** Ask scale first: a set of concrete items → flat; a band that argues two to four claims →
illustrations. Then content (docs/page-build/treatment-rules.md § 1).
