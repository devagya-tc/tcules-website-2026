The drawn visual kind is called an "illustration", in the chapters, specs, briefs, code comments and
replies. Never "drawing". The owner's words: "drawings is not in our vocab".

**Why.** ch05 names three media kinds (capture, cut-out, illustration) and the code's `Media kind`
uses the same names. A second word for one kind makes readers wonder whether it is a fourth.

**The mistake it prevents.** Brand-chapter redlines and agent replies on 24 Sep that wrote "drawing"
for an illustration, as the 16 Sep amendments did ("what bounds a drawing").

**How to apply.** Use "illustration" in every document and identifier. (The kit's small hand-drawn
visuals have their own approved name, Sketch, and are not illustrations.)
