A case study cover's cut-out sits on a background chosen for the set, not on `--color-paper` or
`--color-paper-deep`: a colour, tone or texture that lifts the UI and elevates the covers is allowed. In the
owner's words: "i don't mind him doing something that elevates the design. the same paper/paper-deep color
will look like there's no bg to the cutout." The background fills the whole canvas, and one finish (radius,
shadow, spacing, background treatment) holds across the whole set.

**Why.** A cover on the page's own ground has no edge of its own: on a paper card the cut-out reads as if it
has no background at all, and the set loses its weight as imagery.

**The mistake it prevents.** The first draft of Rishabh's handoff constrained every cover to paper or
paper-deep, following ch05 §9a, which would have shipped 20 covers that dissolve into the card.

**How to apply.** Case study covers (CaseCard, selected-work, the case index) at 16:9, exported as PNG with
the background included, no transparency. Still holds: cut-outs only, no screenshot, no logo or type laid
over (§9a), and ink and blue stay reserved site-wide. Until promoted this is context for Rishabh's
direction, not a rule; the chapter text in §9a (ground) and §5 (our assets sit on the surface's ground)
changes only once it is active.
