`brandeis-900` is not a surface, on any canvas: not on social, not in share previews, not on the
website, and not in chapter 02.

**Why.** Blue's job is to be seen as brandeis-500. A near-navy ground reads as a second dark ground
competing with ink and dilutes the one blue. It had been the argument family's ground on social.

**The mistake it prevents.** A "dark blue" band or frame, or a fifth surface added to the site. The
fix in ch10 §F2 that moved `social-posts-v3`'s hinge to brandeis-900 was reversed by this.

**How to apply.** The site's surfaces stay paper, tint, ink and blue (brandeis-500). No token block,
template or social frame paints brandeis-900 as a ground.
