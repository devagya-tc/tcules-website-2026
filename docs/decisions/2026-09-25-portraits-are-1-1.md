A portrait is a 1:1 square in every placement. The choice was between 4:5 and square; square won.

**Why.** One ratio means one crop is shot and approved, and every placement keeps it. Platforms that
mask profile photos to a circle do it themselves; we never mask (§6 "Masking").

**The mistake it prevents.** A 4:5 portrait on one page and a square on another, each needing its
own crop, or a portrait masked to a circle or shape by our own CSS.

**How to apply.** Shoot square at capture, or compose for a square crop: head and shoulders, eye
line in the upper third, headroom at most 10% (§3). Testimonial, team and guest images render 1:1
with `--radius-0`.
