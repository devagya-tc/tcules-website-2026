Video testimonials are allowed and need a beautiful component (7 Oct). Never more than one per section, and never beside other testimonials (9 Oct).

**Why.** Two videos, or a video among written quotes, compete for the play.

**The mistake it prevents.** A grid of video testimonials.

**How to apply.** VideoTestimonial.
