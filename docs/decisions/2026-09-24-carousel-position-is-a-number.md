A carousel shows its position as numerals in the counter role, current over total (`02 / 07`), from
frame two on. No progress device: no squares, bars or dots.

**Why.** A number says exactly where the reader is and how far is left; a row of squares has to be
counted. The "counter's squares at 10px" note in §A2 was deleted with this ruling.

**The mistake it prevents.** Dot or bar progress indicators on social frames, which read as
platform chrome and cannot be counted at a glance.

**How to apply.** The counter sits opposite the wordmark in the Line action row of every content
frame. The cover carries none. (On the website, the kit's `Slider` is governed separately.)
