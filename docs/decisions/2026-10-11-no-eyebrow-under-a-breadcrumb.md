On a page with a breadcrumb the hero eyebrow stays empty. The crumb already gives location.

**Why.** An eyebrow under a crumb repeats it, and the pair looks like an island.

**The mistake it prevents.** Service pages showing the section name as both crumb and eyebrow.

**How to apply.** PageHero on every crumbed page. Pages without a crumb may use the eyebrow as a content slot (e.g. a rating badge).
