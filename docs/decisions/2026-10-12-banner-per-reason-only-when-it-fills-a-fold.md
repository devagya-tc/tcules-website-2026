Give each reason its own banner only when it fills a fold (a claim, a paragraph and a quote, as on home). Reasons of a sentence or two take one section of 16:9 image cards.

**Why.** Otherwise each banner is a short caption floating beside a big photo. Owner: "idk what but this looks bad".

**The mistake it prevents.** The healthtech "why" band as a run of near-empty banners.

**How to apply.** Choose the form by how much each reason carries.
