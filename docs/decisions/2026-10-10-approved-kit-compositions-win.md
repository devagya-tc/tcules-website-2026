Previously approved kit compositions win over a re-reading of a ruling. Get the design fundamentally right first ("beauty first"); never let content shape excuse a bad composition.

**Why.** Pass 1 split the logo wall from the Clutch rating that kit/home had combined, and the owner hated it. "We completely lost the beauty aspect"; "take my message as feedback, not as instructions."

**The mistake it prevents.** Re-deriving an approved composition from the letter of a later ruling, as pass 1 did with the logo wall and rating.

**How to apply.** Before changing an approved kit composition to satisfy a rule, check the approved kit look (/design/kit/*). A critic judges beauty before rule compliance.
