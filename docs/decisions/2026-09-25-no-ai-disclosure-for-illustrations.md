Generated illustrations are not disclosed: no "Illustration generated with AI" caption, and no
site-level note either. The rule lives in ch05 §10 (AI-generated imagery).

**Why.** The question was whether each caption appends a disclosure or the site carries one
site-level note. The owner chose neither: an illustration is drawn to the house standard and judged
as the studio's own work, and the caption's job is to say what the image establishes (§7).

**The mistake it prevents.** The 16 Sep amendment list flagged §12's open disclosure question and
noted that a site-level note would need a slot nothing had. Building that slot, or appending a
disclosure line to captions, is now ruled out.

**How to apply.** `Figure` captions and alt text say what the illustration shows and never how it was
made. No component grows a disclosure slot or footnote for generated imagery.
