Section openers built on a photograph, with a display line over it, are permitted on the website
for about, careers, studio and culture, served by a dedicated section-opener pattern rather than by
`PageHero`.

**Why.** "It is the only place in the chapter where an image may set a tone rather than prove a
claim, and about/careers are where the site being text-first hurts most."

**The mistake it prevents.** Reading §6's "no component takes an image behind text" as a blanket ban
and leaving about and careers with no photographic opening.

**How to apply.** Withdrawn on 24 Sep by `2026-09-24-no-image-carries-type`. The opener pattern was
never built, and about and careers open with a photograph as a block, beside or below the h1.
