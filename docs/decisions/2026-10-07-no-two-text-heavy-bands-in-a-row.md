No two text-heavy sections back to back. A band with no photograph and a body of words is followed by a visual one.

**Why.** Consecutive walls of words are the plain page the kit exists to replace.

**The mistake it prevents.** Service pages stacking prose bands.

**How to apply.** The ServicePage plan warns in development (`textHeavyPairs`); it is a warning, not a build check.
