Client work never appears on an Instagram story, and a frame carrying client work is never reshared
to one.

**Why.** A story is seen for a couple of seconds at arm's length, and the platform's 240px bands and
link sticker leave no room for an element at its own scale. The work cannot be read, so it cannot
prove anything.

**The mistake it prevents.** Resharing a case-study carousel frame to a story, where the cut-out
shrinks below its scale floor under the platform chrome.

**How to apply.** Stories carry story-first frames or reshares of non-client content only (ch10 D1).
