A critic loop is briefed on the foundations it must not override: section heads at h2, no counts in navigation, no ornamental numerals. The loop optimises beauty, not checklists.

**Why.** After the beauty loop the critics had shrunk section heads to h4 ("too small, foundationally incorrect"), added counts and a CURATE label to the rail, and put 01–04 numerals in lists ("messy"). All were reverted. PR #112 was "made functional but not beautiful" because the loop optimised counts, dates and fold.

**The mistake it prevents.** PR #112 and the 16 Oct beauty loop.

**How to apply.** Every critic brief lists the logged rulings for its area as constraints, and a critic must judge beauty, not only rule compliance.
