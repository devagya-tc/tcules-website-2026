A project's cover or banner may be product UI, as a cut-out, or an illustration. The rule is that it
conveys the project's concept, not that it is never UI. What is banned is a whole-screen screenshot.

**Why.** A draft had read "covers convey the concept" as "covers are always illustrations". Where a
part of the product is what changed, a cut-out of that part carries the concept better than a
drawing of it.

**The mistake it prevents.** Illustrating a cover whose story is a shipped screen, or the opposite:
a full-screen screenshot standing in as the cover.

**How to apply.** Use §9a's decision order: product UI when UI is available, what changed is part of
the product, and the cut-out passes both checks (nameable at 326px, one shape at 160px). Otherwise
an illustration from the archetype registry.
