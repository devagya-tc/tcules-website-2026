"We shouldn't be drawing illustrations for case study cards, they should use the existing image for a case
study." A case without an image shows the placeholder until one exists. A project cover or banner may still
be product UI as a cut-out, never a whole-screen screenshot (carried from the entry this supersedes); what
it may no longer be, on a case study card, is an illustration.

**Why.** Real work is the proof; a drawing of it argues nothing a buyer can check.

**The mistake it prevents.** The isometric "many become one" drawings drawn as the four case covers on the
product-design page (mock round 1).

**How to apply.** Case cards take `coverFor` (the case's own image, else the register's placeholder).
Drawings belong to items that have no real image and whose idea needs showing.
