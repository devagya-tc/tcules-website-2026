Type smaller than 28px is Public Sans, applied by role: h5, h6 and quote-sm are sans everywhere, while h1–h4 and the larger quotes keep Newsreader at every width.

**Why.** Codified the 15 Oct "serif only in titles" by role rather than by instance.

**The mistake it prevents.** Serif at h5/h6 or in small quotes.

**How to apply.** Superseded 19 Oct by `2026-10-19-only-display-h1-h3-serif`, which moved h4 to the sans.
