Under the three engagement cards the two shared terms sit as one plain row at body size, on the grid, with no plates,
drawings, lines or nodes. The space between the cards and the row separates the tiers.

**Why.** The rail of lines and nodes read as a timeline; the drawn terms read as items four and five.

**The mistake it prevents.** Decorating a tier to make it read as shared.

**How to apply.** EngagementOptions renders terms as text only, under the set.
