Proof items are logos, case studies, testimonials and the platform rating. They are a set, NOT a ranking: none is numbered, ordered by strength, or billed as "top".

**Why.** Claude's first pass read the review as an order of proof.

**The mistake it prevents.** Proof sections ordered or labelled by strength.

**How to apply.** Draw on proof as the argument needs; CLAUDE.md § Design quality carries it.
