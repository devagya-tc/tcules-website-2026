The small "AI" badge stays in illustrations. It sits only on what an AI produced, only on AI pages, and
it is the one lettering an illustration may carry beyond ch05 §4b's annotation.

**Why.** Round 3's blind tests read AI pages without it as "ordinary approval or undo screens". The two
letters are what make the AI visible.

**The mistake it prevents.** Dropping the badge for "zero reading" and losing the AI from the AI Product
UX pictures (AI-UX B4 and C2).

**How to apply.** The badge goes on the AI's output (a draft, an action, a pointer), never on the person's
control. It doesn't appear on non-AI pages. ch05 §4b gains it at the next `/dbf-update`.
