**Claude merges the no-decision tasks** (2.1–2.5, 2.7, 2.11, 2.14, 2.16) once every check passes and the
screenshots show nothing unexpected. **The owner merges the ★ tasks** (2.6, 2.8, 2.9, 2.10, 2.12, 2.13,
2.15).

**Why.** Nothing should wait on the owner except a decision. A task with nothing to decide gains nothing
from their merge.

**The mistake it prevents.** Mechanical foundation work queuing behind owner review, or a task that
needed an owner decision merging without one.

**How to apply.** In Phase 2: unstarred tasks merge on green checks and clean shots; starred tasks stop
at the PR for the owner.
