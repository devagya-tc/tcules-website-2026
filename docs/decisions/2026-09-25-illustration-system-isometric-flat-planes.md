The illustration system is the one the owner wrote into ch05 §4a–§4d on 25 Sep: flat planes in
parallel projection (2:1 axes, 26.57°), drawn in one line weight; blue draws the system, with one
solid blue plane; peridot marks the one "carried item" that travels through it; and the annotation
layer is a concept chip plus two part labels. No figures, no gradients, shadows or new hues.

**Why.** The owner rewrote the section themselves, so its wording is the ruling. Placed-by-formula
construction is what keeps every illustration in one projection and one family.

**The mistake it prevents.** Illustration rules restated from memory or an older draft, which drift
from what the owner wrote; hand-nudged perspective; a second accent or a person drawn as a body.

**How to apply.** Re-read ch05 §4a–§4d before drawing or briefing any illustration, and before
touching an illustration rule. Never paraphrase them into a spec; link the section.
