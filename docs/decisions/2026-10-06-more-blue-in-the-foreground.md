The owner wants MORE blue in the foreground: icons, links, markers, numerals and small marks, so the site does not read black and white. "Reserved" applies only to grounds: no surface is ever blue. "Wisely" means coherent, not sparse.

**Why.** Claude read "used wisely" as "used less".

**The mistake it prevents.** The fdn-colour pass that moved blue at rest to ink.

**How to apply.** Default foreground accents to blue. ch04 §5 says "Blue on an icon beside neutral text" is a don't, and ch02 §8 allows a blue band per page.
