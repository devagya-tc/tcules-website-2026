Neatness is judged closely: connectors attach at an axis or an edge midpoint (a cylinder connector aligns to a disk's centre, not an arbitrary point); arrowheads never sink into a fill of their own colour; draw by depth so occlusion is right.

**Why.** Loose anchoring reads as careless at hero size.

**The mistake it prevents.** Connectors meeting shapes at arbitrary points.

**How to apply.** `scripts/illustrations/` generates the SVGs; the review page has a read-back mode.
