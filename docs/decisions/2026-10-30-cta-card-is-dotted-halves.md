The mini CTA card that ends a tile set has a dotted outline and no fill. With two questions it splits into two halves of
one structure each: the question, its sentence, then its destinations as arrow links. No outline button inside, so both
halves read the same.

**Why.** A filled card with a button in one half and links in the other read as two different things.

**The mistake it prevents.** Mixing control styles inside one small card.

**How to apply.** One question: one half's structure across the card. Columns per 2026-10-30-set-ends-in-a-cta-card.
