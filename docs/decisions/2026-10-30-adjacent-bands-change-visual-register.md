The registers are: flat graphics, isometric illustration, IRL photograph, proof (case covers, client marks)
and figures. Two adjacent bands never use the same one, the way two text-heavy bands never touch.

**Why.** Variety of arrangement is not enough when every band draws in the same register; the squint test sees
one idea.

**How to apply.** The service page runs: hero illustration → selected work (proof) → What you get (flat +
photo) → hard parts (illustrations) → services (flat tiles) → testimonials (quotes) → How we work together
(flat) → Why Tcules (illustrations) → industries (photos) → enquiry island. The page plan warns in development
on an adjacent pair, as it does for text-heavy pairs.

**The mistake it prevents.** A page where four bands in a row draw in flat graphics, each correct alone.
