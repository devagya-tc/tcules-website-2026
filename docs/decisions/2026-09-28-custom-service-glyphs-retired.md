The six custom service glyphs in `Icon` are **retired.** They are not Solar Bold (ch04); service lines get
illustrations instead.

**Why.** ch04 allows one set, Solar Icons Bold, and forbids redrawing, tracing or combining glyphs.
Bespoke glyphs were outside the system.

**The mistake it prevents.** Hand-drawn service icons living in `Icon` beside Solar glyphs, in a second
style.

**How to apply.** `Icon` carries Solar Bold glyphs only (chrome marks excepted). A service line is marked
by its illustration, not a glyph.
