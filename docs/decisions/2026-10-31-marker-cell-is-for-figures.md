"i don't like this card component for content. it's good for stats and figures." The ruled cell with the short
blue marker (OfferSheet's item) holds a figure and its label. Product kinds, deliverables and claims never sit in
it.

**Why.** On What you get and What we design it set lower-case sentences as a uniform grid with no shape of its
own; "this could be visualized so so much better."

**The mistake it prevents.** The expertise What we design and What you get rendered as OfferSheet / ReadDown cells.

**How to apply.** What you get uses OfferParts; What we design takes a flat drawing per product kind (to design;
visual-inventory-expertise § 2). The record line of figures keeps the cell.
