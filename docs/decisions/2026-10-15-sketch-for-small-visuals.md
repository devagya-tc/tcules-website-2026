"The drawing style wasn't a problem we can continue using that and keep the illustrations reserved for larger more detailed concepts." The Sketch scenes (16:9 plate, dot field, quiet frames, one blue subject) are the visual for selectors and cards. The isometric line illustrations are kept for heroes, articles and covers.

**Why.** The owner's complaint about the home selector was the craft of colour, type, layout and prominence, not the drawings.

**The mistake it prevents.** Redrawing six isometric scenes for the home situation selector.

**How to apply.** Use `Sketch` for small per-item visuals; do not redraw sketches when a section's craft is criticised. ch05 §4 knows only the isometric style.
