A section that routes to services exists so the ICP can choose quickly. The item title (their situation, their reason) is the hero. Imagery must not out-shout it; service illustrations are the wrong imagery there.

**Why.** Owner: the gateway was "focusing more on the images than on the actual services".

**The mistake it prevents.** The home gateway led by large service illustrations.

**How to apply.** Any routing band. All items are visible together in one fold so the reader can scan for theirs.
