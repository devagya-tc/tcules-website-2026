Facts and figures belong in the hero, not in a band of their own.

**Why.** A band of numbers alone is a weak band and repeats what the hero should be saying.

**The mistake it prevents.** A standalone figures band mid-page.

**How to apply.** Put a page's figures in its PageHero.
