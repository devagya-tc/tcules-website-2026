Never put a structure-only build in front of the owner. Once references and layout are agreed, make 2–3 high-fidelity visual directions for that one section as static mocks (an HTML artifact or Figma), with drawings, type, colour and interaction states designed in full. The owner picks one; only then is it built in the repo.

**Why.** The owner reviews everything as finished design. A layout of stock parts (grey tiles, existing sketches, a plain paragraph with a link) is a wireframe, and every review judged the section without the layer meant to carry it. Owner: "ugh no this is all still bad design."

**The mistake it prevents.** Rounds 9 (grid) and 10 (tiles plus preview) of the home gateway, both built in code with the drawings left for later.

**How to apply.** Applies to every section redesign. Never present grey boxes or placeholder visuals as a design.
