No blue bands for now. A blue band needs a set, justified purpose, and none exists yet.

**Why.** A blue ground without a job is decoration.

**The mistake it prevents.** A blue pivot band added for rhythm.

**How to apply.** Section surfaces. ch02 §8 allows "one band per page at most, usually the ask". The enquiry close built on PR #113 sits on blue and is logged there as an open exception.
