The ground does not tell evidence, argument, people and invitation apart. The mono eyebrow naming
the content type does, with each family's default row pattern. No colour coding.

**Why.** The 24 Sep grounds ruling took ink and brandeis-900 away as family grounds, so "four grounds,
four families" could not survive. The addendum called what replaces it the biggest thing left
unsettled; the owner settled it on 25 Sep.

**The mistake it prevents.** Re-introducing a ground or accent per family as a signal, which spends
the reserved grounds as a rhythm device.

**How to apply.** Every frame's eyebrow names the content type. Each family keeps its default row
pattern (B3). Ground stays paper-led whatever the family.
