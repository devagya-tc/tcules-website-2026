Texture is **kept as a decorative ground element**, placed where it lifts a composition, within the
limits in the component audit §3: never under an image, never under body text or a heading, never on ink
or blue (reserved), at most one per page, and never as a rhythm device between bands.

**Why.** ch05 §5–6 bans treatments on and behind images, so the boundary is the image, not decoration
as such. A ground element can lift a composition without breaking that rule.

**The mistake it prevents.** Removing Texture as "decoration" on the next pass, or spreading it behind
text, images and every other band.

**How to apply.** `Texture`'s spec records the limits; a placement is checked against them by eye. Superseded 5 Oct by `2026-10-05-texture-for-rhythm`: texture may be used wherever it improves page rhythm.
