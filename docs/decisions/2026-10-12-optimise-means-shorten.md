When asked to optimise length, keep every section, its order, its heading and its items, and cut each to a sentence or two. Never merge, split or rename sections. Owner: "optimize length and not change the narrative or structure".

**Why.** Optimising was read as licence to restructure the page.

**The mistake it prevents.** The about-ideal pass that merged and renamed sections while shortening.

**How to apply.** Any content-length pass on a page or corpus file.
