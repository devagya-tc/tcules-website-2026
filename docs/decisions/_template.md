The ruling, in one or two sentences, stated as the rule rather than as the story of how it was reached.

**Why.** The reason the owner gave or the evidence that forced it. A ruling without its reason gets
re-litigated the first time it is inconvenient.

**The mistake it prevents.** What was actually built, or nearly built, that this rules out. Concrete:
a component, a page, a PR. This is the line a future session reads to recognise it is about to repeat it.

**How to apply.** Where it bites: which components, tokens, templates or steps of the workflow.
