A set of points never sits as a separate strip under a split.

**Why.** Detached from the copy they belong to, the points read as another section.

**The mistake it prevents.** CtaSection's landscape offer, removed for this reason.

**How to apply.** Any split with a list of points: the points go inside the copy column.
