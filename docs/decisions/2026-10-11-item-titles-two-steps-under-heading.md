Item titles sit two type steps under their section heading (h2 → items h4). Listed items get breathing room too: a 24 inset per row, not 16.

**Why.** At one step (h2 43 with items at h3 34) six titles competed with the heading.

**The mistake it prevents.** Round 8 of the home gateway, with h3 item titles.

**How to apply.** Any band with a heading over titled items. ch03 §5 shows two compositions but states no step rule.
