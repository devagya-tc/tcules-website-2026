Stop arranging content shapes. "Think about beauty. Don't limit yourself to one card or one layout… think as a visual designer not as a functional designer. Think textures, graphics, visuals, in addition to the fundamentals." Every house rule still holds (paper/tint grounds, no shadow, no overlap).

**Why.** Every round of the selected-work section re-arranged the same four boxes (cover, claim, quote, speaker). The owner called PR #111's one-card TestimonialCase and three mock directions (Plate, Panorama, Overlap) "all horrible": the section's problem was visual poverty, not arrangement.

**The mistake it prevents.** Rounds that shuffle the same boxes into a new grid and present that as a new design.

**How to apply.** Start from a visual idea (material, texture, graphic system, how the client work is staged), then fit the content into it. References are shown as images (a moodboard of real screenshots), never as word descriptions; Claude finds them.
