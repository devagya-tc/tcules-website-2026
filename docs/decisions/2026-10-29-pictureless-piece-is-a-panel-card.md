In the finder's results, a piece with no picture is a panel: a box the cover's size and shape (1:1
from 768), on the page's own paper with the image's hairline and radius, holding the format's glyph and
name at its head with the date at the line's end, and the title, body (three lines at most) and topic
tag pinned to its foot. So a row mixing pictured and unpictured pieces reads as one set.

**Why.** "Build a boxed card variant to show items without images in the grid so it looks nicer."
"Here's where we can add some more info to compensate for a lack of an image." Its ground: "keep the
paper bg, paper-deep is too much." 74 of 98 insights have no picture, and only 3 a description.

**The mistake it prevents.** A results grid of titles floating on paper beside cover cards; ruled rows
(built and dropped: bare, with almost no standfirsts); a panel on paper-deep, too heavy in a row of
them; a panel that declared its own tint surface just so the tag would show.

**How to apply.** Only where pieces of mixed kinds share one grid (the results). The curated view still
shows only pictured pieces. This does not reopen 2026-10-05-cards-have-no-boxes: the panel stands in the
cover's place and keeps the hairline an image keeps; it is not a box around a card's words.
