A worked example's steps (the problem, what we did, the result) read by type and spacing alone: no rail
down their start edge and no rule above each one, and no blue rule marking the result. "One main issue is
the lines on the left of the 3 items or in our current version the lines above the 3 items. i particularly
don't like that part of the design."

**Why.** The owner's dislike of the device itself; it is also a rule that separates nothing the order and
spacing do not already separate (2026-10-16-lines-only-where-they-structure).

**The mistake it prevents.** CaseSpread's rail (hairline turning blue at the result) and the strip's ruled
step tops on the product-design page's Dealpath example.

**How to apply.** CaseSpread, both arrangements: the steps a `block` apart, the result last and unmarked.
The --csp-rule-* and --csp-claim-rule-* tokens are gone.
