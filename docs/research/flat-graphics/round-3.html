How the motion works

Choreographed, not animated

  • One gesture, 250ms. The whole drawing is one movement, and every part of it finishes at the same instant, 250ms after it starts.
  • A wave, not a queue. The order you saw in take 1 (route, then nodes, then the focal point) survives only as when each part joins the movement: within the first 140ms. Everything then settles together.
  • Deceleration, not speed. Lines and slides start fast and spend most of their time easing into place (an exponential ease-out). Nothing moves at a constant rate.
  • Things that land, spring. Ports, nodes, arrowheads and the focal point scale up with a small overshoot. Slides are short (12 to 20 units), so the lens, the cursor and the people arrive rather than travel.
  • Once, on entering view. Never on hover, never looping, nothing under reduced motion.
