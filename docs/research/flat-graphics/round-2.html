Graphics · tracker 2.26 · redrawn, round 2

Composed, not assembled

The ten Sketch scenes (the seven services and the three article covers) redrawn to the six rules, each beside the light-weight version that is on the site now. Same plate, same colours, same story per scene.

Round 2. Round 1 added words to the drawings (tab labels, node names, button text, line numbers) and they read as diagrams. The words are gone; the icons stay, because a lock or a person reads at a glance and a word has to be read.

The rules as drawn

What every scene now follows

1 · Routes

Every connector leaves the middle of an edge, runs on the axes, turns on one 6-unit arc and ends on a port or the one arrowhead. None crosses a shape: where a route has to pass a frame's edge it gets a port there.

2 · The grid

Ports, turns, nodes and line ends sit on the field's dots (every 20 units, from 10). Frames start and stop on the dot lines.

3 · Weight follows height

1 for every outline, 2 for copy and the subject route. What floats (the lens, the cursor) takes a darker 2 to 2.5 outline with a flat offset under it.

4 · No improvised marks

People, locks, flags and sparkles are Solar glyphs. Arcs are true arcs; the cylinder is true ellipses. One arrowhead form.

5 · No words, real marks

No text inside a drawing: the words beside it carry the meaning. Detail comes from real pieces instead: Solar glyphs (a lock, a person, a flag, the stars) and real UI parts (a toggle, a checkbox, a cursor).

6 · Accent at the joints

Blue is the route, its ports and the one lit node. Everything else is grey or paper.

New product

The whole product dashed, its first release built solid, the launch lit.

Product design

A screen whose one flow is drawn through it, the decision lit.

  • The diagonal zig-zag becomes a real flow: a step, a decision, two named branches.
  • Both branches turn on the one radius and end on the same arrowhead.
  • The decision is the only solid shape, so the eye lands there first.
03 · product-modernisation

Product modernisation

The cluttered screen, then the same screen clean.

04 · design-systems

Design systems

Your call

What to decide on this round

  1. Do the drawings read as pictures again, or does any still feel like a diagram? Product design (a step, a decision, two branches) is the one most at risk.
  2. The lift. The lens and the cursor take a darker outline and a flat grey offset. Is that the right way to float, or too much?
  3. The glyphs. The flag, lock, people, stars and layer icons are the detail that replaced the words. Keep all of them, or fewer?

Once these read right, the same rules go to the other three families (Engagement options, Trace plates, Engagement model) and then into the shared token layer.
