# Flat graphics: the drawing grammar (checkpoint, round 2)

**Checkpoint:** 25 Oct 2026, tracker 2.26. Context for the next round, not a ruling. Nothing here is applied to
the site yet; the rules become a ruling (via `/decide`, `status: proposed`) when the redrawn set is built and
seen.

**Where to look:** the mock artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/WsuuMmh9H7swKXuW5gcZ9j (version 2), also saved
here as `flat-graphics/round-2.html`. The ten Sketch scenes are generated by `flat-graphics/scenes.py` (glyph
paths in `glyphs.json`, copied from `src/components/Icon/glyphs.ts`).

## How we got here

1. The owner ruled out monochrome illustrations (proposed: `decisions/2026-10-25-two-graphic-tiers-no-monochrome`,
   on branch `design/reasons-no-peridot`). Two tiers: flat graphics and peridot isometrics.
2. Which tier a piece of content takes is decided by the content, never by the component: an object or a single
   concept takes an isometric (heroes, and the Why tcules set); a surface (a screen, a flow) takes a flat
   graphic. Page rhythm breaks a tie: neighbouring bands don't share a style.
3. The owner liked the home bento's thin strokes over the kit's heavy ones, and asked for polish: "if connectors
   are drawn they cant simply be lines from a to b", "not every stroke has to be the same weight", no shabby
   arcs, "composed not assembled".
4. References (26 product sites, artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/S6VP2cPe5EnmTrY2b1GzMa) gave six rules.
   The owner accepted all six, with no single reference to follow.
5. Round 1 added words inside the drawings (tab labels, node names, button text). The owner: they "read as some
   kind of a diagram. i think the text is hurting more than helping." Round 2 removed every word.

## The rules as they stand

1. **Routes.** A connector leaves the middle of an edge, runs on the axes, turns on one 6-unit true arc and ends
   on a port or the one arrowhead (filled, 7 long, 8 wide). It never crosses a shape; where it passes a frame's
   edge it gets a port there.
2. **The grid.** Ports, turns, nodes and line ends sit on the field's dots (every 20 units, from 10).
3. **Weight follows height.** 1 for every outline, 2 for copy and the subject route. What floats (a lens, a
   cursor) takes a darker 2 to 2.5 outline (neutral-700) on paper, with a flat offset under it (neutral-200).
4. **No improvised marks.** People, locks, flags and sparkles are Solar Bold glyphs; arcs are true arcs; one
   arrowhead form.
5. **No words, real marks.** No text inside a drawing. Detail comes from glyphs and real UI parts (a toggle, a
   checkbox, a cursor).
6. **Accent at the joints.** Blue is the route, its ports and the one lit node.

Corners: 8 for a screen, 4 inside it, pills stay pills. Dash 6 4. Colours unchanged from `--skt-*` paper.
