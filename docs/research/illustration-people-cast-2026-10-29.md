# How people are drawn: Jatin's "Tcules Cast" exploration, 29 Oct 2026

**Status:** context for the next illustration pass. It is not a ruling. Devagya shared it on 30 Oct: "keep it
stored as context and I might use this in the next pass of illustrations."

**Source:** https://claude.ai/artifact/QAgCEDk2pRYFYkcNq57Fn9. It is an exploration made for Jatin, who said on
the 30 Oct call that Jatin was working on "how a Tcules person looks". Every reader and reviewer in it is
simulated: AI agents briefed as B2B buyers, a brand art director, a production engineer and an evidence auditor.
No real buyer has seen any of it.

**Already ruled, so it frames this:**
- People are part of the illustration rules (`decisions/2026-10-30-illustrations-carry-people`).
- The AI badge stays (`decisions/2026-10-30-ai-badge-on-ai-output`).
- **The accent is peridot and it marks the one carried item, "as it has been".** The exploration calls the colour
  "lime" and uses it as "the decisive point, once per picture". Read its colour advice through the peridot
  ruling, not the other way round.

## The starting fact

Of the 37 banners with people, about 8 show a Tcules person. The other 29 show the client's team, their users or
their customers. So "Tcules people stand out" can't mean making every figure louder; that would push the buyer out
of their own picture. The bolder treatment belongs to the few Tcules people, and everyone else stays light.

## M, the converged option

- **Tcules person:** plain solid blue, no texture, leaning slightly into the work. **The habit:** always a
  dot-tipped fingertip on the work, "tap here". A blind reader picked it over leaning and peering as the most
  personable, professional and memorable.
- **Client's people:** pale and upright, with a firmer blue tint so they don't read as "hollow ghosts", and
  never lesser. They get an arm when they are the one acting.
- **Designer working with AI:** the same solid figure, with a small diamond linked to the working hand by a dotted
  line. Sparkles on a body read as "an AI assistant in human form", the wrong message for buyers worried about AI
  replacing designers.
- **AI agent alone:** a diamond, never a human body. It hovers with a soft shadow, not on a stalk. **Open:** its
  four-point star reads as Google Gemini to every reader. The alternatives are a network mark, which read as AI
  only "maybe" and looked like a road sign, or a Tcules-drawn symbol, which would need its own test.
- **Hand:** Jatin's E hand at 78% width with a 2px line, plain white, no knuckle or fold lines, no wrist band and
  no selection box. The press is a small ring on the surface under the fingertip, not on the hand.
- **Delivery:** banners ship as vector or lossless images. JPEG and WebP compression removed every accent pixel
  from a figure at phone size.
- **Icons:** they share the rules, not the drawings: line weight, corners, blue, one active part. Only the three
  people icons (Tcules person, client, agent) borrow from the cast. At 24px an icon must never depend on the
  accent.

## Where it conflicts with the rules, for whoever picks it up

- **The accent:** M keeps "lime exactly once per picture, always edged in blue; people never carry lime". That
  agrees with peridot being scarce, but it treats peridot as a focal mark. The ruling is peridot as the carried
  item, so a pass using M re-reads its colour lines that way.
- **The soft shadow** under the hovering agent: ch05 §4a has no shadows.
- **The dotted link** from hand to diamond: per `decisions/2026-10-23-illustration-lines-follow-iso-axes` it runs
  on x, y or z, and per `2026-10-30-connectors-end-at-the-edge` it stops at the edges of the hand and the diamond.
- **The figures already on the pages** (Bhumika's finals, polished 30 Oct) use a solid blue pawn for the Tcules
  person and an outline pawn for others. That is close to M's solid/pale split, but without the lean, the
  fingertip habit or the diamond.
