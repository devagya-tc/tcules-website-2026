# Product design careers at Tcules in Ahmedabad

Tcules is a digital product design studio in Ahmedabad, working since 2017 with software teams in 19 countries. We hire designers and engineers who want to understand why a product behaves as it does, and to take on more as their judgement grows.

[Apply for a role](#apply)

## Serious product work, without leaving Ahmedabad {#ahmedabad}

You shouldn't have to move cities to find demanding product work and strong peers. Our clients are spread around the world; the team works together in our Ahmedabad studio, our home since 2022.

Sharing a room changes how the work gets made. A designer can pull an engineer into a browser problem. Someone at the next desk can question a workflow while it is still easy to change. Critique happens during the work, as well as in review.

## Understand the product, then make it better {#product-work}

Projects range from new products and mature SaaS workflows to design systems, AI features and frontend code. Whatever the brief, we expect you to ask what the user is trying to do, what the product already knows and where the exceptions hide.

As you grow, the questions get bigger. You move from designing a screen well to framing the problem, leading the conversation with the client and helping the team choose what deserves building. Engineers are expected to know why a feature exists as well as how to build it.

## How AI fits into the job {#ai-fluency}

AI is part of how the studio works, and that changes what a strong practitioner needs to know. You will learn when a task can go to a tool or agent, and what it needs. Just as important: how to check what comes back, and when to do the work yourself.

The aim is to put your attention on the parts of a product that need judgement. Taste, writing, interaction design and visual craft still decide whether the work is good.

[Read how AI-native Product Design works at Tcules](/how-we-work/ai-native-product-design)

## What helps people do well here {#what-we-look-for}

- Noticing what is really going on before reaching for a solution.
- Explaining a choice plainly, and taking good critique well.
- Curiosity about unfamiliar fields.
- Care for real content, states and edge cases.
- Knowing where AI tools tend to go wrong.
- Ownership, with progress shown as you go.

A design degree helps. It is one route among several.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Are Tcules roles remote or in-office?

The current full-time roles are in-office at our Ahmedabad studio. If a future role works differently, its listing will say so.

### Do you hire early-career designers and engineers?

Yes, when a suitable role or Lab place is open. Full-time roles and Future Interfaces Lab opportunities are listed separately, each with its own terms.

### Do I need to be an AI specialist?

For most roles, no. Practical AI fluency helps everywhere, and the depth you need depends on the responsibility the role carries.

### What should a design portfolio show?

Your reasoning. Show the problem, the constraints, the options you weighed, the choice you made and what changed when you learned something new. Real states and edge cases tell us more than polished hero screens.

### What matters for engineering roles?

Understanding what the product is for, working well inside an existing codebase, and taking responsibility for software that works for its users, beyond the ticket in front of you.

## Apply for a role {#apply}

Email [hello@tcules.com](mailto:hello@tcules.com) with the role you want and a link to work that shows your reasoning.

No matching role? If your work feels unusually relevant, write anyway. It won't start a hiring process, but we will know where to find you when the right role opens.
