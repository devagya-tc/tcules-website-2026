# Product design videos

Full-length recordings of our conversations with people who build software products. Each video plays on YouTube, and each has a written summary if you would rather skim first and watch the parts you need.

## Watch the recordings {#recordings}

**[Build AI for the user's work, not for AI's sake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QNz1zr3U7Zo).** Samy Melaine of Eden AI explains why giving developers many AI models in one place means making the choice between them simpler, and how his engineers and designers shape each change together. [Read the summary](/insights/podcasts/do-ai-not-for-ai-s-sake-but-for-your-user-insights-from-eden-ai-s-ctpo).

**[Making product decisions across evidence and disciplines](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=44Nta0CkcOY).** Parasharan Chari, Dhimant Trivedi and Akshay Vyas on hearing from customers, why a much-used feature may still be a poor one, and releasing part of a design without losing the rest. [Read the summary](/insights/podcasts/mastering-design-decisions).
