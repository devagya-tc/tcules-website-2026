# Product design events

Talks and meetups we have hosted for designers, engineers and product people, written up so you can take the useful parts without having been in the room.

**[Design systems for everyone, from basics to advanced](/insights/events/design-systems-for-everyone-from-basics-to-advanced).** Our March 2023 meetup in Ahmedabad, introduced as the city's first on design systems. It covered auto-layout, design tokens and composable systems, with a hands-on exercise.

**[When a product designer moves to Bollywood](/insights/events/product-design-and-filmmaking).** Arastu, who moved from product design into filmmaking in Mumbai, on how a film travels from idea to audience. Participants tried a group filmmaking exercise.
