# When a product designer moves to Bollywood

Tcules invited Arastu, who moved from product design and a startup in Ahmedabad into filmmaking in Mumbai, to explain how filmmaking turns an idea into coordinated creative work. The session gave product practitioners another way to examine disciplines, hand-offs, interpretation and what happens when a finished piece meets an audience.

By Tcules · Published 2 May 2025 · Updated 8 September 2026

Filmmaking offered a different way into familiar questions about creative work: how an idea becomes something a team can make, how each discipline changes it and what happens when the result meets an audience. Those are useful questions for a studio that takes product ideas through design and implementation.

The session included a group filmmaking exercise, giving participants a chance to try the process together.

The session was listed for 2 December, 11am to 1pm. The event notice does not state the year.

We make room for conversations outside product design because another discipline can reveal choices we have stopped questioning. The working method that comes out of that attention is described under [how we work](/how-we-work).
