# Preksha Kharidia, Product Designer at Tcules

Product Designer, Tcules

Preksha Kharidia is a Product Designer at Tcules who writes about SaaS onboarding and engagement, with Yash Bhatt.

The two co-written guides cover how SaaS customers reach value and keep finding it. The onboarding guide maps how the buyer, the administrator and the daily user each reach a first real result after sign-up. The engagement guide starts by defining the repeated behaviour that shows value. Trials, personalisation and gamification come after.
