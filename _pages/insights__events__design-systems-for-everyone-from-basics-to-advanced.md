# Design systems for everyone, from basics to advanced

Tcules hosted a design-systems meetup in Ahmedabad in March 2023, bringing the discussion from shared foundations to practical work with components. We introduced it as the city’s first meetup dedicated to design systems. The session moved from the mechanics people can adopt immediately to the decisions that only matter once a system has more than one consumer:

By Tcules · Published 11 March 2023 · Updated 8 September 2026

- auto-layout, and what it actually buys a team that has been resizing frames by hand;

- design tokens, and the difference between a named colour and a decision that holds its meaning across products;

- composable systems, where the question stops being which components exist and becomes which combinations the system is willing to allow;

- a hands-on exercise to apply the ideas during the session.

The Design Systems for Everyone event page collects the session topics and related reading.

The reasoning behind the material is written up under [design systems](/services/design-systems), and the question of what a system should own before anyone builds it is [design system strategy](/services/design-systems/design-system-strategy). If a system already exists and the question is why teams work around it, that is a [design system audit](/audits/design-system-audit).
