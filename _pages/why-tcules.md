## Six things to look for in a product design partner {#buyer-tests}

### 1. They question what the product assumes

Good product design starts below the screens: who the users are, what they're trying to do and what the software takes for granted. At Doodle, research reshaped the MVP for an enterprise scheduling product. At Dealpath, one button assumed every listing should become a brand new deal.

> “Impressed with the design team's efficiency. Not a rush job, but constant iteration with our feedback. Collaborative approach led to a clear, well-defined MVP. Early users are happy, so are we!”  
> **Stephanie Leue, Director of Product, Doodle**

[See our Product Design work](/services/product-design)

### 2. They're at home in complex software

Enterprise software is hard because its rules, roles and exceptions all have to fit together. Auxentios' quoting product had to serve sales, product, solution, pricing and maintenance specialists on the same quote. We mapped who contributes what and where work is handed on, then designed one shared quoting workflow around it.

> “We were impressed with their lead designer and team because they understood our complex concepts.”  
> **Ashok Mohanty, Founder, Auxentios Technology**

### 3. They'll push back on the brief

Sometimes the requested redesign leaves the real problem untouched. We bring research and product thinking in early, and tell you when the brief is pointing the wrong way.

<!-- awaiting client sign-off -->
> “Tcules told us what was really wrong, including the things we didn't want to hear, and then fixed them with us.”  
> **Akshay Vyas, Creative Head, Knovos**

### 4. They can work inside your team

We work with your product managers, designers and engineers directly, in your tools, and share our thinking as we go.

> “It was simply the most successful long-distance 3rd party collaboration I have seen. The company is on eye-level or above for the services they offer. Their staff were really good.”  
> **Roland Sailer, Director of Product, Ringier Media Switzerland**

### 5. Their design reaches the code

The work can carry on into coded prototypes, frontend components or a full build. For Benchmark Gensuite we built the new screens as working prototypes in code, so the team could try them before rolling them out. For GT Tools we designed and built the Shopify store.

### 6. Their work holds up to a closer look

Ask what changed in the product, what the studio actually did and what the client said. Then check the case studies, the reviews and the people you'd work with. [Explore our case studies](/case-studies)

## Which kind of partner do you need? {#partner-comparison}

| If what you're missing is… | Consider | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Someone to own the product long term | Hiring in-house | Years of embedded knowledge matter most |
| One well-defined task with a settled direction | A freelance specialist | One expert, light coordination |
| Engineering capacity against a finished specification | A development company | Build speed and scale matter most |
| Product thinking across research, design, systems and code | **Tcules** | One team takes the product from first idea to working software, so nothing is lost between research, design and build |

Pick the partner that fills the gap in your team. We're happy to tell you if that's someone else.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### How should we compare product design agencies?

Ask each one to walk you through a hard product decision: what they learned, what changed, who did the work and what reached the build. Then read their client reviews and meet the people you'd work with.

### Can you help if we already have designers?

Yes. We can take on a defined project, add research or design skills your team lacks, set up a design system or help your team carry work into code.

### Can you take on a complete first release?

Yes, when the engagement includes engineering, testing and release. [See Software Development](/services/software-development)

### Can we start small?

Yes. An assessment, a pilot or one product question is a good way to see if we fit.

### When should we choose someone else?

If you mainly need low-cost screen production, a fixed marketing website or a large engineering team with no product design responsibility, another kind of provider will suit you better.
