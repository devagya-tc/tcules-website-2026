# Tcules cookie policy

*Last updated: 20 October 2026*

This policy explains how Tcules UX Private Limited ("Tcules", "we", "us" or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website, and how you can control them. It sits alongside our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

## What cookies are {#what-cookies-are}

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit. They let the site remember things, such as your choices, and help the people who run it understand how it is used.

Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in much the same way. In this policy, "cookies" covers them too.

## The cookies we use {#how-we-use}

We group the cookies on our website by what they do.

- **Strictly necessary.** These make the website work and keep it secure. They also remember the cookie choices you make. Because the site needs them, we set them without asking.
- **Analytics.** These help us count visits and see which pages people use, so we can improve the website. We set them only if you agree.
- **Preferences.** Where we use them, these remember choices you make on the website. We set them only if you agree.

Some cookies are set by the service providers that run these features for us, as our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy#sharing) describes. If we start using cookies of another kind, we will update this policy and ask for your agreement before setting them.

## Your choices {#your-choices}

We set cookies other than strictly necessary ones only after you agree. You can change your choice at any time through the cookie settings on our website. If you decline, you can still use the website.

The cookie settings list the specific cookies we use, with what each one does and how long it lasts.

## Controlling cookies in your browser {#managing}

Most browsers let you see, block and delete cookies. You will find these options in your browser's settings or help pages.

If you block all cookies, some parts of our website may not work as expected. If you delete cookies, your cookie choices are deleted too, so you may need to set them again.

## Your personal information {#personal-information}

Some cookies collect information that can identify you, such as your IP address. Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) explains how we handle it and the rights you have.

## Changes to this policy {#changes}

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the cookies we use change. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

If you have questions about how we use cookies, write to us at hello@tcules.com, or at Tcules UX Private Limited, 614, Silver Radiance 2, Sola, Ahmedabad, India.
