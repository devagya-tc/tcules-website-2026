# Future Interfaces Lab: experiments from the Tcules studio

Most of our work happens inside client projects. The Lab is where we run our own experiments: prototypes, internal tools, datasets and learning programmes that explore how AI is changing the way software is designed, built and used.

[Join the Lab](/future-interfaces-lab/join)

## Why the Lab exists {#purpose}

Some questions are too early for a client project but concrete enough to test. Experiments need room to change shape, or to show that an idea was a dead end. The Lab gives that work a home.

## Experiments in how design work gets done {#design-work}

Questions here include: can AI help review a product without dulling a designer's judgement? Can one description of an interface pattern serve people and machines?

### Matter Design System

A free Figma design system we made and published, with purpose-named tokens and components built from smaller parts. [Explore Matter](/matter-design-system)

### AI Design Audit Agent

Tests how much of a first-pass design review AI can help with, while keeping what was observed separate from what it means and what to fix first.

### UI Pattern Vision Labelling Tool

A labelling tool that asks whether interface patterns can be described in a form designers recognise and machines can work with.

### Landing Page Audit Programme

A practice programme that makes conversion critique more explicit: less personal preference, more about what the page shows, what that costs and what to change.

## Experiments in how people use AI products {#ai-interfaces}

Questions here include: how should a voice interface handle mishearing? How can a shopping assistant stay clear when the shopper is unsure? How can work carry over when it moves between AI models?

### Open Canvas Workflow Builder

A workflow experiment for knowledge work across several AI models, where progress and reasoning have to carry over between tools and views.

### Gujarati Voice AI Math Tutor

A spoken maths tutoring experiment for Indic languages. It explores how recognition confidence, accents, hesitation, correction and pacing shape the conversation between a learner and the tutor.

### Shopping Assistant Programme

A commerce experiment in AI shopping help, where search, follow-up questions and recommendations stay clear as the shopper changes their mind.

## What earns a place in the Lab {#threshold}

Each item needs something real to look at: a prototype, a working tool, a dataset, a reusable design file, a documented experiment or a learning programme with a clear goal. Ideas we haven't built stay off the list.

Some work is public, some we can demonstrate on request, and some stays internal. Anything public carries its own reuse terms.

## When an experiment becomes everyday practice {#promotion}

When something keeps proving useful, it moves out of the Lab. A method joins [How we work](/how-we-work), a public aid becomes one of our [tools](/tools), a lasting definition goes into the [glossary](/glossary), and work for a client becomes a [case study](/case-studies).

## Build inside the Lab {#join}

People who want to work on Lab projects can apply through the [Lab application page](/future-interfaces-lab/join), which shows what is open now and on what terms.

## Talk to us {#talk}

Want to work on an experiment with us, or talk about your own product? Send a note through our [contact page](/contact).
