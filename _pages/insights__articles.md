# Product design articles

Twelve articles from Tcules designers and the product people we interview. They cover AI inside products, design systems, prototypes on their way to production and the everyday design of SaaS products. Each one takes a position and says what a team should do about it.

## AI inside the product {#ai}

**[Confidence scores don't fix trust when the review step never returns control](/insights/confidence-scores-dont-fix-trust-when-review-step-never).** Why adding a confidence score, a warning and a review step can leave usage flat, and how to design the moment a person takes control back.

**[AI interfaces need calibrated trust, not maximum transparency](/insights/articles/building-trust-and-transparency-in-ai-first-user-interfaces).** How an AI feature should show its role, its sources, its authority, its uncertainty and the way to recover, with more review as the stakes rise.

**[Seven generative AI product patterns for SaaS](/insights/articles/generative-ai-product-patterns-for-saas).** Generate, transform, retrieve, recommend, predict, orchestrate and act: what each pattern asks of the product, and the control each one needs.

## Design systems {#design-systems}

**[When to hire a design system consultant, and what they should deliver](/insights/when-to-hire-a-design-system-consultant).** When outside help is worth paying for, when it is the wrong call, and what a good consultant should leave your own team able to run.

**[Make the design system useful in product decisions](/insights/articles/design-systems-for-saas-boosting-efficiency-and-ux-consistency).** Follow one missing component state from a product team's request to a shared release, and see what that journey says about the system.

## Prototypes, releases and quality {#quality}

**[I vibe-coded an app. Now what? The path from prototype to production](/insights/vibe-coded-app-production-ready).** What an AI-built app still has to answer before real users arrive: what it stores, who can see each record, what happens when a step fails and who fixes it.

**[Customers can finish the task and still lose confidence](/insights/articles/silent-ui-regression-debt).** Four ways an interface decays between releases while completion rates look healthy, and the question to ask before the next one ships.

## Designing SaaS products {#saas}

**[CPQ UX: designing configure-price-quote software sales teams will use](/insights/cpq-ux-design).** Configuration rules that explain themselves, prices and approvals visible before submission, and revisions that keep earlier work: a practical guide with a checklist.

**[Cognitive bias is a design risk, not a bag of conversion tricks](/insights/articles/cognitive-biases-in-ecommerce-and-saas-products).** Start with the bias in your own team's reading of the data, then test a behavioural explanation before designing around it.

**[UX, CX and design thinking name different things](/insights/articles/design-thinking-vs-cx-vs-ux-explained).** What each term covers, where they overlap, and how to tell which one is big enough to explain the problem in front of you.

## Interviews: My Experiments with UX {#interviews}

Conversations with people who ship products about how AI is changing the way they design and decide.

**[Karthik Pasupathy on context, clarity and core functionality](/insights/articles/my-experiments-with-ux-karthik-pasupathy).** Fyno's then head of marketing on how technical writing, product analytics and AI changed the way he approaches product work.

**[Aishwarya Ashok on building an AI-first product experience](/insights/articles/my-experiments-with-ux-aishwarya-ashok).** How she took the visual collaboration product VaniHQ from idea to beta at Zoho, and why she researches where people complain.
