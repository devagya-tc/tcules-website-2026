# Product design guides

Eight practical guides for teams working on SaaS and commerce products. Each takes one job, such as onboarding, an audit or a landing page, and walks through how to do it well.

## Getting SaaS customers to value {#saas-value}

**[SaaS onboarding should lead each role to evidence of value](/insights/guides/saas-onboarding-strategies-to-increase-customer-retention).** Define first value separately for the buyer, the administrator and the daily user, then map each one's route from sign-up to real work.

**[Define valuable behaviour before optimising SaaS engagement](/insights/guides/saas-engagement-and-subscription-decisions).** Work out which repeated behaviour shows a customer getting value before reaching for trials, gamification or personalisation, and diagnose why usage fell.

**[Does your B2B SaaS product need a mobile app?](/insights/guides/saas-does-your-app-need-a-mobile-version)** Find the moments of work that happen away from a desk and cannot wait, then choose the mobile product shape that suits them.

## Running campaigns and operations {#operations}

**[Design marketing automation around decisions, not dashboards](/insights/guides/marketing-automation-software-design-best-practices).** Show operators who receives what, after which signal, under which rule and with whose approval, before any message goes out.

## Reviewing and improving a product {#improve}

**[A product audit should end in decisions, not observations](/insights/guides/how-to-use-a-ux-product-audit).** Choose the question before the method, look beyond the screens, and write each finding so a team can own it, sequence it and test it.

**[Inclusive product design for older and disabled people](/insights/guides/inclusive-product-design-for-older-and-disabled-people).** Pair a testable accessibility baseline with the people who meet the barriers, and remove one barrier without creating another.

## Commerce and conversion {#commerce}

**[Improve commerce by finding the broken evidence path](/insights/guides/ecommerce-product-experience-decision-guide).** Trace where shoppers lose confidence between discovery and delivery, and measure the whole path, returns and support included.

**[A landing page is one continuous product decision](/insights/guides/landing-page-design-guide-to-boost-conversions).** Design from the ad, email or search result that brought the visitor, through the page, to the confirmation after they act.
