*How we work*

# Work with the people doing the work

We join your product team, work in your tools and keep you close to every big choice. You talk directly to the designers, researchers and engineers on the project, and always know what happens next.

[Start a conversation](#enquiry)

**Studio in Ahmedabad, India · Clients in 19 countries · Direct access to the people on your project**

## Some of the teams we've worked with {#client-recognition}

Ringier Media Switzerland · JoVE · Doodle · Eden AI · Dealpath · Benchmark Gensuite

## Four ways to work together {#engagement-shapes}

### Find out what needs to change

A [UX audit or focused assessment](/audits) shows you where users get stuck, what to fix first and what to do next, before you sign up for anything bigger.

### Solve a defined problem

A feature, a workflow, onboarding, a redesign or a first release, with clear goals and deliverables.

### Add designers to your team

Experienced Tcules designers work inside your product, design or engineering team. You keep the product direction and the final say.

### Design and build through to release

Product Design, [Design Engineering](/services/design-engineering) and [Software Development](/services/software-development) in one engagement, including build, testing and handover.

Engagements can change shape as you learn. Any change to scope, roles or cost is agreed openly.

## How a project moves {#working-loop}

| Stage | What we do with you | What you can see |
|---|---|---|
| **Understand** | Learn your users, product, constraints and team | A shared brief, what we found and the open questions |
| **Choose** | Explore options, show the trade-offs, agree priorities | Flows, scenarios and the reasons behind each choice |
| **Make** | Research, design, prototype or build what was agreed | Screens, states, components and working prototypes |
| **Review** | Test with users, stakeholders or in the browser | Feedback, changes and sign-off |
| **Hand on** | Release, hand over or keep going together | Files, documentation, code and a clear list of what's next |

Projects move back and forth between these stages when they need to. What matters is that nothing gets lost along the way.

## Talk directly to the people doing the work {#practitioners}

You speak with the designers, researchers and engineers on your project, and review each choice with the people who made it.

The studio works together in Ahmedabad. For international clients we agree working hours that overlap with yours, and keep everything else visible in shared files and clear written updates.

## You see the thinking, as well as the result {#artifacts}

A complex workflow might need a map of who can do what. A risky interaction might need a coded prototype. A design system needs both Figma and coded components. We make whatever helps your team understand the reasoning and act on it.

See it in real projects: [Doodle](/case-studies/doodle-case-study-automating-enterprise-meeting-scheduling), [Benchmark Gensuite](/case-studies/benchmark-gensuite-enterprise-suite-modernisation) and [GT Tools](/case-studies/gt-tools-shopify-product-discovery).

## When design carries on into code {#design-to-release}

Some projects end with designs your own engineers build. In others we work in your frontend code, deliver a finished interface or build a defined part of the application.

Either way, we agree the details at the start: repository access, components, reviews, tests, deployment, security sign-off and what stays with your team.

## How we use AI in our own work {#ai-practice}

AI is part of how we research, explore options, prototype and check our work. People make the product decisions and review the work before it is delivered. If your organisation has rules about AI tools, tell us during scoping and we will agree an approach for the project.

[How we use AI at Tcules](/how-we-work/ai-native-product-design)

## Who owns what {#shared-ownership}

| Tcules owns | Your team keeps |
|---|---|
| Quality and delivery of the agreed work | Business and product strategy, and final priorities |
| Making trade-offs, open questions and risks visible | Product knowledge, stakeholders and access |
| Engineering work we have agreed to build | Release sign-off, business risk, and security and compliance decisions |

A good partner leaves you with clear files and knowledge, so your team can carry on without us.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Who will we work with day to day?

The designers, researchers and engineers doing your work, with specialists joining when the project needs them.

### How do you handle confidential information?

We agree confidentiality and data rules with you before work starts, and our tools and people work within them. If you need an NDA before sharing anything sensitive, tell us when you get in touch.

## Tell us who we'd be working with {#enquiry}

Your team, your tools and what needs to ship. We'll suggest how to fit in.
