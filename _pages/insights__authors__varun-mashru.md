# Varun Mashru, Product Designer at Tcules

Product Designer, Tcules

Varun Mashru is a Product Designer at Tcules who writes about CPQ software UX: designing the configure-price-quote tools that sales teams use to build and send quotes.

The CPQ article treats the quote as the one record everyone works on. It covers who contributes and with what authority. It also covers rules and price changes that explain themselves. Approvals show before anyone submits, and a quote can be revised without starting over.
