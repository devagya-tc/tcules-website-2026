The result someone picks is where a knowledge platform earns its place: the procedure an engineer follows, the passage a researcher cites, the answer a newcomer gets without knowing the term. Eden AI's developers could start a workflow from a template, by asking the AI or from scratch, and each route led somewhere slightly different. ## Where knowledge platforms and search get hard {#hard-parts}

Search problems hide in logs and in people's habits. Search quality is partly the engine and partly the screen, and labels, previews, filters and empty results do much of the work.

### Which version is current, and who still owns it

Search returns six versions of the same policy, and nobody can tell which one is current or who approved it. Pages go stale because nobody owns them. We put status, owner and date in each result, where they help someone choose, and flag pages past their review date to their owner.

Internal knowledge bases carry their own rules. Quality standards such as ISO 9001 ask for controlled documents, with a current version, an approver and a record of changes. Publishers run a second product, the one editors work in, where a correction has to reach every copy.

Example, for what a result shows: Eden AI's results became preview cards that link straight to the documentation. A developer can judge a capability before adding it, with the deeper detail a click further in. [Read the Eden AI case](/case-studies/eden-ai-workflow-builder-search-redesign)

### AI answers people can trace to a passage

People need to tell a quoted source from the AI's own summary, and to open the exact passage an answer came from. Knowledge bases are also becoming the source that AI assistants and agents read, so owners, versions and review dates now have to be readable by machines as well as people.

Research platform design adds the reader who cites what they find. A researcher needs the exact passage, where it came from and whether it has changed since they last looked.

Example: in our design for BuildTwin's review, every AI check links to its drawing reference and source document, and a check that cannot run says which information is missing. [Read the BuildTwin case](/case-studies/ai-quality-control-expert-review-buildtwin)

### The expert's exact name and the newcomer's description

Experts type the exact name. Newcomers can only describe the problem. Separate searches for each look tidy until an expert meets an unfamiliar machine, and one box that guesses fails the moment nobody can see why it guessed.

Example: GT Tools' navigation and search were reworked for buyers who know a part number and for buyers still comparing equipment. [Read the GT Tools case](/case-studies/gt-tools-shopify-product-discovery)

*Illustration, not client work*

A new product helps maintenance engineers find the right page in a manual. A veteran types a part number; a newcomer types *pump making a grinding noise*.

We would design one box that recognises a part number and goes straight to it, and reads anything else as a description, matched against the words senior engineers hear newcomers use. The product asks which machine first, then shows the likely pages with the matching passage.

## Questions about knowledge platform design {#faq}

### Can you improve search without replacing the engine?

Yes. Much of the trouble sits in the interface: how the search box suggests and corrects queries, how results are labelled, filtered and previewed, and what happens when nothing matches. Tcules redesigns those, and ranking and indexing stay with your engineers.

### How should AI answers show their sources?

Close to the answer, with a way to open the exact passage or document. People should be able to tell a quoted source from the AI's own summary. Our article looks at [how much an AI interface should show before people can trust it](/insights/articles/building-trust-and-transparency-in-ai-first-user-interfaces).

### How do we show which version of a document is current?

With the fewest signals that answer the question: status, owner and date. Older versions stay available but out of the way, so a superseded policy is harder to pick by mistake.

### Do you design publisher and media platforms?

Yes. Publishers share the same core problems: archives that outgrow their search, articles with several owners and readers who need the current version. We design the reader experience and the editors' tools together, so a correction reaches every place it should.

### Who designs knowledge and content discovery platforms?

Product design studios that treat search, sources and versions as one product. Tcules designed the search and documentation inside Eden AI's platform, BuildTwin's AI review feed and GT Tools' product discovery, and designs internal knowledge bases, document libraries and publisher platforms.
