# SaaS software development from the team that designs it

Our designers and engineers build SaaS products, web apps and major features together, so details agreed in design carry into the code.

[Start a conversation](#enquiry)

*Selected work: Vovisto · GT Tools · The Wandering Raven · Gold Image Printing*

## What do you need built? {#recognition}

### A new SaaS or web product

From an idea or a prototype to a first release customers can use. [New Product Design & First Release](/services/new-product-design-first-release)

### A big new feature in a product you already run

New workflows, account features, integrations or AI features, built to fit the product your customers know.

### A frontend that matches the design

Responsive interfaces, every state and reusable components that behave in the browser the way they were designed. [Frontend Development](/services/software-development/frontend-development)

### APIs and integrations

Data flows, logins, business logic, third-party platforms and what happens when they fail. [Backend & API Development](/services/software-development/backend-api-development)

### Ongoing development

New features, fixes and improvements for a live product, from a team that already knows it. [Product Maintenance & Evolution](/services/software-development/product-maintenance)

## What we agree before we build {#scope}

Every build starts with four clear answers.

| | What we agree |
|---|---|
| **What it should do** | Users, roles, journeys, rules and edge cases |
| **What it touches** | Frontend, backend, data and integrations |
| **When it's ready** | The testing and checks it must pass before release |
| **Who owns what** | Code, reviews, infrastructure, security and support after launch |

So you know exactly what you're getting, and so do we.

## How a build runs {#delivery-model}

**1. Plan.** Agree the users, the goal, the priorities and who decides.

**2. Design.** Settle the important workflows, states and components.

**3. Build.** Frontend, backend and integrations, with designers involved throughout.

**4. Test and launch.** Test against what was agreed, fix what needs fixing, then hand over or keep improving it with you.

## From design into working software {#proof}

### Vovisto: a custom platform for healthcare event travel

Vovisto runs sponsorship and travel for healthcare professionals attending medical conferences, work that depended on manual coordination across people, bookings and approvals. We mapped the roles, approvals and exceptions, and designed the platform, including a portal for healthcare professionals. We then led the move into development, coordinating the engineering, supplier API integrations and testing. The platform is now in implementation and integration testing, and we also maintain it.

### GT Tools: a Shopify store built around how buyers search

We redesigned and built GT Tools' Shopify store so buyers can check compatibility, variants, stock, shipping and warranty on the product page, with the price and buy button always in view.

[Read the GT Tools case](/case-studies/gt-tools-shopify-product-discovery)

We designed a creative-writing experience for learners aged eight to eighteen and built it into Moodle, with selected plugins, configuration and a character-led look.

## Why build with a product design studio {#why-tcules}

**Designers and engineers work side by side.** Designers check each build against the agreed design, so details survive the move into code.

**Edge cases get handled before they become bugs.** Roles, permissions, data and failures are designed, then built.

**Your engineers or ours.** We can build the whole product, join your team in your repository, or take on one clearly defined part.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Can you build our whole first release?

Yes. We design and build complete first releases: frontend, backend, integrations, testing and launch.

### Can you work in our existing codebase?

Yes. We can join your engineers in your repository and workflow, or take on a clearly defined part of the product.

### Do we have to start with a discovery phase?

No. We reuse the research, specifications and code you already have, and only investigate what is still unclear.

### What happens after launch?

Your choice. We can hand everything over to your team, or keep developing and maintaining the product with you.
