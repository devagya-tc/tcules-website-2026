# PropTech and construction software design for deal teams and engineers

From real estate acquisition at Dealpath to AI drawing checks at BuildTwin, we design property and construction products where one asset looks the same everywhere and every sign-off shows its source.

[Start a conversation](#enquiry)

*Selected work: Dealpath · BuildTwin · Benchmark Gensuite*

## Where property and field software falls behind the work {#hard-parts}

**"The same property looks different on every screen."** Dashboard, map and list each show it their own way, so people second-guess what they are looking at.

**"Issues get flagged without the drawing or photo behind them."** Reviewers have to chase the source before they can act.

**"Our field teams find sites by where they are standing."** An organisation chart is no help in a car park when you are looking for site 14B.

**"The AI flagged it. Can the engineer disagree?"** Automated checks need a person who can overrule them, with a record of why.

## How we design for property, construction and field work {#approach}

**One asset, one identity.** A property, project or site keeps the same name, status and key figures wherever it appears, from a full dashboard to a map pin.

**Evidence travels with the issue.** Drawings, documents, photos and notes stay attached to the finding they support.

**Each role sees its part.** The person who prepares, the person who reviews and the person who approves each get the actions and detail they need.

**Designed for the site as well as the office.** Finding a place by where you stand, and picking a task back up after an interruption, are part of the brief.

## Designed for desks and building sites {#proof}

### Dealpath: a listing card that answers "is this one worth a look?"

Dealpath is software for real estate acquisition teams, who scan many listings to decide which deserve closer attention. We kept all the property data and rearranged the card so the first glance answers that question, with the figures for detailed comparison below.

A property keeps that layout on the dashboard and on the map, so it looks the same wherever it appears.

[Read the Dealpath case](/case-studies/enterprise-workflow-redesign-dealpath-real-estate)

### BuildTwin: AI drawing checks with an accountable engineer

In BuildTwin's structural-engineering software, a drawing passes from the detailer who prepares it, to the quality engineer who reviews it, to the approver who moves it on. BuildTwin was adding AI checks to the middle of that chain.

We redesigned the review so the engineer sees which drawing and document each check relied on, can supply missing information, and can raise an issue when they disagree.

[Read the BuildTwin case](/case-studies/ai-quality-control-expert-review-buildtwin)

### Benchmark Gensuite: finding the right site from the field

Benchmark Gensuite makes environment, health, safety and sustainability software for large organisations. Its users pick where they are working from the organisation's hierarchy, which suits a desk better than a site visit.

Our redesign added Nearby, a view that lists sites by the user's location, alongside browsing and recent or favourite places. We tested it in a coded prototype.

[Read the Benchmark Gensuite case](/case-studies/benchmark-gensuite-enterprise-suite-modernisation)

## Why property and construction teams work with us {#why-tcules}

**We keep what experienced users rely on.** Property and field teams build habits around their tools, so we change what causes trouble and leave familiar routines alone.

> “The team was skilled and knowledgeable in the workflow.”  
> **Eduard Stinga, Founder, VideoPlasty**

**We design for accountability.** When people sign off work on a building, the product shows what they checked and why they decided as they did.

## Questions about PropTech and field products {#faq}

### Do you design tools for people working on site?

Yes. People on site need to find a location by where they stand, record what they see and pick up a task after an interruption, and we design for each. Benchmark Gensuite's Nearby view, above, is one example.

### Can you improve one workflow inside an established property platform?

Yes. At Dealpath we redesigned one listing workflow to fit the existing product and its technical limits, then handed it to Dealpath's engineers ready to build.

### How do you design AI inspection and review tools?

Every AI result shows what it is based on, missing inputs are flagged, and a qualified person can overrule the result with a reason that stays on record. That is the review we designed for BuildTwin.
