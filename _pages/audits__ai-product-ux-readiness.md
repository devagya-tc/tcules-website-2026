*AI Product UX Readiness review*

# Check your AI feature before more people rely on it

An AI feature can work and still leave users unsure what it did, what to check and how to undo it. We review it against real tasks and tell you what to fix first.

[Start a conversation](#enquiry)

*Selected work: Omny AI · BuildTwin · Eden AI*

## Signs your AI feature needs a second look {#recognition}

**"Users check every answer twice."** Or they have stopped checking at all, which is worse.

**"Support keeps getting asked what the AI did."** People can see that something changed, but not what, or why.

**"We're about to switch it on for every customer."** A friendly pilot group liked it. Everyone else will use it differently.

**"It used to suggest. Now it acts."** The AI can change records on its own, and nobody has mapped how to undo that.

## What we check, task by task {#coverage}

| The question | What we look for |
|---|---|
| What is the AI doing for the user? | Whether people can tell if it is finding, suggesting, drafting or acting |
| What can people check? | Sources, reasons or comparisons shown where the result appears |
| What may it do alone? | Which actions need approval, and whether the product makes that obvious |
| What is the reviewer's job? | Whether the person approving knows what to look at and what their approval changes |
| How do people fix a wrong result? | Editing, rejecting, retrying and undoing, and how easy each one is to find |
| Who owns the outcome? | Whether anyone can see who accepted a result, and when |

We use the running feature on tasks your users actually do, including the awkward ones where the AI is unsure or plain wrong. Feedback and support tickets show us where to look first.

## What you get at the end {#deliverables}

- **A ranked fix list:** where each problem appears, who it affects and what it costs them.
- **Fixes users would notice,** such as a clearer label, a visible source or an undo.
- **Questions for your specialists** on accuracy, security or compliance.
- **A walkthrough with your team** to agree what comes first.

## What we changed in three AI features {#proof}

Each of these redesigns had to answer the questions this review asks.

**Omny AI.** AI-written product benefits were going into Amazon listings before anyone had properly read them. We moved suggestions into their own panel, made adding one a deliberate click, and marked the AI's work in purple and people's work in blue.

Omny is one of the Primelis products we design. [Read the Omny AI case](/case-studies/omny-ai-suggestion-trust-redesign)

Your team can work from the list on its own. Or our [AI Product UX](/services/ai-product-ux) designers can redesign the parts we flag, and our engineers can build the changes through [AI Product Engineering](/services/software-development/ai-product-engineering).

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Is this a test of the AI model's accuracy?

It tests the experience around the model: what people see, check, approve and undo. Accuracy testing, security and compliance belong to your engineers and specialists. When we spot a problem in their area, we pass it on with the example that showed it.

### Can you review an AI agent?

Yes. With agents we look hardest at what the plan shows before it runs, which actions wait for approval, how someone stops it halfway and how they find out afterwards what it changed.

### Do you give the feature a score?

You get a ranked list instead of a single number. A score hides which problem to fix first, and that is the question your team needs answered. Each finding says who it affects and how much it matters.

### What do you need from us?

Access to the feature with realistic data, a few tasks your users rely on it for, and any feedback or support tickets about it. A short call with the people who built it helps us understand what it is meant to do.

### Is a beta too early for this review?

A beta with real users is a good time. Changing how the AI shows its work or asks for approval costs less before everyone has learned the current version, and your beta group's feedback shows us where to start.
