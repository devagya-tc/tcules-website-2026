*Glossary · Design System Governance*

# Design system governance

Design system governance is the decision and ownership model through which shared assets are proposed, evaluated, released, migrated and retired. When it matters: Governance decides what happens at the moment a product needs something the system does not have. Without it, contribution stalls and forks become the default.

Suppose two products need a new status pattern. Governance determines the evidence of reuse, accessibility effect, owner, release path and migration obligation. It should make legitimate contribution possible without forcing every product-specific need into a shared pattern.

Governance can be central, federated or hybrid. The useful model is the one that fits the organisation's product boundaries and decision rights. [Design-system adoption](/glossary/design-system-adoption) shows whether that model works in product delivery.

Explore [Governance and Adoption](/services/design-systems/governance-adoption) or establish the operating model through [Design System Strategy](/services/design-systems/design-system-strategy).

## How is design-system governance used in practice? {#practice}

Design-system governance determines how a shared product decision enters, changes and leaves the system. A team needs to know who can propose a change, who decides whether it belongs in the shared layer, how the component is reviewed and released, and how consuming products move to the new version. The process should make the shared route easier than repeated local workarounds.

Tcules treats governance as part of [Design Systems](/services/design-systems), not as a policy document separate from product delivery.

## How does governance differ from documentation? {#comparison}

The table separates instructions about the system from the mechanism that changes it.

| Documentation | Governance |
|---|---|
| Explains how a component or pattern should be used | Defines how shared decisions are proposed, accepted, released and maintained |
| Can describe the current state | Assigns decision rights and a route for changing that state |
| Helps a consumer apply the system | Helps contributors and owners evolve the system |

## Why design system governance matters in product work {#next}

Governance is the operating path: who decides, who contributes, how exceptions are recorded, and how a release reaches products.
