# Pending, approved or stuck? Your customers would like to know.

FinTech product design for lending and payments: statuses that mean one thing, identity checks that explain themselves and failures that come with a way forward.

[Start a conversation](#enquiry)

*Selected work: OctiFi · Merchant payments research*

## The questions your customers are asking {#hard-parts}

Every unanswered question in a financial product turns into a drop-off or a support call.

| Moment | What the customer is wondering |
|---|---|
| Signing up | "Why do you need all this, and how long will it take?" |
| Applying | "Will I qualify, and what exactly am I agreeing to?" |
| Waiting | "Is it pending, approved or stuck?" |
| Paying | "Did that go through, and when will it arrive?" |
| Making a claim | "What do you need from me, and when will I hear back?" |

## How we design financial products {#approach}

**Every status means one thing.** Pending, approved, funded, failed and reversed each get a plain label, a short explanation and a next step. Customers and staff read the same status the same way.

**Checks are explained when they are asked for.** Identity checks and document requests say why they are needed, and progress is saved for people who stop halfway.

**Failures come with a way forward.** A declined application, a failed payment or a missing document shows what happened and what the customer can do now.

**Staff screens get the same care.** Review and approval tools show who can approve, what they are looking at and what changes when they act.

**Your specialists set the rules, and we make them usable.** Credit policy, compliance and legal requirements come from your team. We design how customers and staff meet them.

## Lending and payments products we've designed {#proof}

### OctiFi: a lending-app pilot that grew into wider design work

OctiFi ran a mobile lending app in Singapore and was preparing for a second Southeast Asian market. What began as a short design pilot went on to cover onboarding, loan issuance, new features and adapting the app for that market.

We worked through each flow in rough wireframes first. High-fidelity prototypes with realistic data followed, so OctiFi's product, engineering and marketing teams could review the experience before committing to code.

> “They deliver the works required on time. They deliberate sufficiently and outline clear thought processes in the flows.”  
> **Amin Nordin, Head of Growth, OctiFi**

[Read the OctiFi case](/case-studies/octifi-mobile-lending-product-design)

### Merchant payments: twenty interviews before any build

A client planning a payment product for kirana stores, India's neighbourhood shops, wanted to know which problems were worth building for. We interviewed twenty merchants who matched the intended users, alongside stakeholder interviews and competitor, app-store and social research.

The result was a set of product priorities, concept material and a UX strategy that product and marketing could share, ready before development began.

## Questions from FinTech teams {#faq}

### Can we hire FinTech designers to work inside our team?

Yes. Tcules designers can take on a defined part of your product or work across it with your product and engineering teams, as they did at OctiFi.

### Do you make sure our product is compliant?

Your compliance and legal specialists decide what the rules require, and we design to those requirements. That covers what to ask for, when to explain it and how to show the result.

### Do you design insurance products as well?

Yes. Quotes, policy changes and claims raise the same questions as lending: what am I agreeing to, what state is my case in, and what happens next.
