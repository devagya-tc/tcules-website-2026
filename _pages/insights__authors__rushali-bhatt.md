# Rushali Bhatt, Product Designer at Tcules

Product Designer, Tcules

Rushali Bhatt is a Product Designer at Tcules who writes about AI product UX: how to design review steps in AI features so that people keep control of the output.

The article looks at why adding a confidence score and a warning notice can leave usage flat. It shows where a review step should hand control back to the person using it. It ends with a way to find that point in your own feature.
