# Tcules authors: the product designers and founders who write Insights

Every Tcules Insights piece carries its author's byline. Each profile below lists the articles, guides and podcast conversations that person has published.

## Tcules Insights writers and the topics each one covers {#writers}

- **[Shoghi Bagul](/insights/authors/shoghi-bagul)**, Product Designer: trust in AI interfaces, generative AI product patterns and design systems.

- **[Rushali Bhatt](/insights/authors/rushali-bhatt)**, Product Designer: review steps in AI features that hand control back to people.

- **[Yash Bhatt](/insights/authors/yash-bhatt)**, Product Designer: landing pages, ecommerce, SaaS onboarding and engagement, product audits, marketing automation and inclusive design.

- **[Rakesh Chitroda](/insights/authors/rakesh-chitroda)**, Co-founder: choosing a product design agency and hiring a design system consultant.

- **[Preksha Kharidia](/insights/authors/preksha-kharidia)**, Product Designer: SaaS onboarding and engagement, written with Yash Bhatt.

- **[Jatin Leuva](/insights/authors/jatin-leuva)**, Founder and CEO: podcast conversations, the My Experiments with UX interviews, and guides on mobile apps and vibe-coded prototypes.

- **[Varun Mashru](/insights/authors/varun-mashru)**, Product Designer: configure-price-quote (CPQ) software for sales teams.

- **[Devagya Sharma](/insights/authors/devagya-sharma)**, Product Designer: how an interface wears down quietly across releases.
