# Product design case studies: SaaS, AI and enterprise software

Real products, real problems and what we did about them. Browse by the situation closest to yours: a new product, an established one, an AI feature, or work we designed and built.

[Tell us about your product](#enquiry)

## Four to start with {#featured}

### Doodle: finding meeting times that will hold

An empty slot in a calendar is no promise someone will turn up. Our research found what makes a meeting time hold, and the MVP was designed around it.

**Our role:** research and product design. [Read the Doodle case](/case-studies/doodle-case-study-automating-enterprise-meeting-scheduling)

### Benchmark Gensuite: modernising an enterprise suite

A large, long-running safety suite gets a new interface, tried first in one busy feature as working prototypes in code before it reaches every team.

**Our role:** modernisation, design systems and coded prototypes. [Read the Benchmark Gensuite case](/case-studies/benchmark-gensuite-enterprise-suite-modernisation)

### ConnectX: an AI building-energy workspace

A building-energy product where asking the AI a question opens a workspace with the right data and tools. We proved it in a coded demo, and that demo is now a production-ready module, running since late 2025.

**Our role:** AI Product UX, a coded demo and the production-ready module. [Read the ConnectX case](/case-studies/ai-native-building-energy-product-connectx)

### GT Tools: a specialist Shopify store, designed and built

An automotive-glass equipment store redesigned and developed on Shopify, so buyers can confirm a tool suits their job before they pay.

**Our role:** product design and development. [Read the GT Tools case](/case-studies/gt-tools-shopify-product-discovery)

## New products {#new-products}

**[Auxentios](/case-studies/auxentios-cpq-product-mvp-design): one quoting product for six specialist roles.** Eight user interviews shaped a shared quoting workflow, and a consistent set of screens, for an industrial quoting product.

**[Novus Insights](/case-studies/novus-insights-ai-survey-product-design): AI drafts, the researcher decides.** MVP design for a survey tool where AI suggests a starting questionnaire and the researcher stays in charge.

**[Kirana store payments](/case-studies/ux-research-for-kirana-store-payment-app): twenty merchant interviews first.** Research with small-shop owners shaped product priorities before the client committed to development. Client name withheld.

**[OctiFi](/case-studies/octifi-mobile-lending-product-design): a pilot that became a partnership.** A small mobile-lending design project grew into ongoing product design across onboarding, loans and a second market.

## Established products {#established-products}

**[Dealpath](/case-studies/enterprise-workflow-redesign-dealpath-real-estate): search first, create second.** We redesigned the listing card and the one action that kept starting new deals.

**[Gold Image Printing](/case-studies/gold-image-printing-web-to-print-erp-modernisation): orders and production, redesigned together.** The online ordering site and the internal production system, designed as one product.

**[Ryzeo](/case-studies/ryzeo-marketing-automation-ux-revamp): research before redesign.** Interviews, analytics and a product review decided what to fix first in a marketing automation platform.

**[Scissors](/case-studies/scissors-staffing-software-object-oriented-ux): clients, candidates and jobs, connected.** Staffing software redesigned around one connected model, so details entered once carry through the rest of the work.

**[The Chain Hut](/case-studies/ux-ui-design-and-development-for-magento-of-a-silver-chain-store): a jewellery store on Magento.** Research, a new site structure, a mobile-first design and a simpler checkout for a UK online jewellery retailer.

## AI products {#ai-products}

**[Eden AI](/case-studies/eden-ai-workflow-builder-search-redesign): one way into the workflow builder.** Templates, AI help and a blank start brought into one starting point.

**[BuildTwin](/case-studies/ai-quality-control-expert-review-buildtwin): AI checks an engineer can challenge.** We redesigned the review so each AI check carries its evidence and engineers can override it with a reason.

**[Omny AI](/case-studies/omny-ai-suggestion-trust-redesign): suggestions that wait for approval.** AI suggestions stay separate from the final listing until a person accepts them.

## Designed and built {#designed-and-built}

**[The Wandering Raven](/case-studies/wandering-raven-edtech-moodle-experience): a learning world inside Moodle.** A creative-writing experience for young learners, designed and built into Moodle.
