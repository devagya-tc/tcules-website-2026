# Rakesh Chitroda, Co-founder of Tcules

Co-founder, Tcules

Rakesh Chitroda is Co-founder of Tcules. Rakesh writes for teams choosing outside design help, including how to choose a product design agency and when to hire a design system consultant.

The agency guide covers the kind of partner a team needs and six things to check before hiring. Its closing advice is to test the fit with a small pilot project.

The consultant article sets out when outside help is worth paying for. It covers what a good consultant should leave behind and how to judge the work.
