# Product design podcasts

Two recorded conversations with people who build software products. Each page sets out the main points in writing and links to the full recording, so you can decide what is worth your listening time.

## Episodes {#episodes}

**[Build AI for the user's work, not for AI's sake](/insights/podcasts/do-ai-not-for-ai-s-sake-but-for-your-user-insights-from-eden-ai-s-ctpo).** Eden AI's CPTO on choosing models, using the roadmap to communicate, and what control looks like when a developer platform reaches a wider audience.

**[Making product decisions across evidence and disciplines](/insights/podcasts/mastering-design-decisions).** A panel with Medkart's founder, Contrado's head of engineering and the creative head of Knovos on listening, reading data, roadmap trade-offs and disagreement between teams.
