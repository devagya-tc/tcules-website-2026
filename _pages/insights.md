# Product design insights

Practical writing on the problems product teams bring us: AI features people can trust, design systems teams use, ageing products and SaaS design. Start with your problem below, or browse everything by format.

## AI inside your product {#ai-product-ux}

For teams adding generated answers, recommendations or automated actions to software people rely on at work.

**[AI interfaces need calibrated trust, not maximum transparency](/insights/articles/building-trust-and-transparency-in-ai-first-user-interfaces).** Five things an AI feature should make visible to the person using it, and why a risky action deserves more review than a routine one.

**[Seven generative AI product patterns for SaaS](/insights/articles/generative-ai-product-patterns-for-saas).** A map of where AI can help inside a SaaS workflow, from drafting something for a person to judge through to acting on their behalf.

Our related service: [AI Product UX](/services/ai-product-ux).

## Design systems {#design-systems}

Building a design system, or wondering why product teams keep working around the one you have?

**[Make the design system useful in product decisions](/insights/articles/design-systems-for-saas-boosting-efficiency-and-ux-consistency).** A simple test for any design system: when a team needs something the library lacks, does the request end in a shared release or another local copy?

**[Design systems for everyone, from basics to advanced](/insights/events/design-systems-for-everyone-from-basics-to-advanced).** Our Ahmedabad meetup on design systems, running from auto-layout and design tokens to composable systems.

Our related service: [Design Systems](/services/design-systems).

## Modernising an established product {#modernisation}

Years of releases have left the product hard to change, and you need to know what to fix first.

**[A product audit should end in decisions, not observations](/insights/guides/how-to-use-a-ux-product-audit).** How to run or commission an audit whose findings a team can own, put in order and check afterwards.

**[Customers can finish the task and still lose confidence](/insights/articles/silent-ui-regression-debt).** The small interface regressions that slip past release checks: retried saves, double-checked results and a product that feels a little less dependable with each release.

Our related service: [Product Modernisation](/services/product-modernisation).

## SaaS product experience {#saas-ux}

For SaaS teams working on quoting, engagement and where their product should run.

**[CPQ UX: designing configure-price-quote software sales teams will use](/insights/cpq-ux-design).** Design quoting software around the quote that several roles contribute to, then judge it by whether reps prefer it to their spreadsheet.

**[Define valuable behaviour before optimising SaaS engagement](/insights/guides/saas-engagement-and-subscription-decisions).** Why fewer sessions can mean a better product, and how to pick the repeated behaviour that shows customers are getting value.

**[Does your B2B SaaS product need a mobile app?](/insights/guides/saas-does-your-app-need-a-mobile-version)** It depends on the work that happens away from a desk and cannot wait, and on whether a phone makes that work better.

Our related expertise: [B2B SaaS and workflow software](/expertise/b2b-saas-workflow-software).
